03 / BEFORE SHIPPING

Run the 4-point check.

Warehouse photos are a decision point. Approve only when the order, shape and visible construction agree.

  1. 01ORDER

    Match the order

    Confirm color, size and selected version against your order notes.

  2. 02SHAPE

    Scan the silhouette

    Compare proportions, symmetry, panels and both sides of the item.

  3. 03DETAIL

    Zoom into construction

    Review seams, print placement, soles, hardware and visible damage.

  4. 04PROOF

    Request what is missing

    Ask for a close-up or measurement whenever a view is unclear.

05 / QUICK ANSWERS

Know before you click.

01 / FAQWhat is a Superbuy spreadsheet?+

An independent directory of product links grouped by category. It helps with discovery; the current product page remains the source of truth for price, options and availability.

02 / FAQWhat does Superbuy officially provide at the warehouse?+

Superbuy's User Guidance describes warehouse inspection, photos for review and 90 days of free storage. Its current fee page lists detailed photos and re-inspection as optional paid services, so ask for a targeted view when the standard evidence cannot answer a specific question.

03 / FAQAre the displayed prices final?+

No. USD amounts are browsing references. Check the exact live variant price, China domestic delivery, any purchasing fee that applies to the source platform, optional services and international parcel cost before ordering.

04 / FAQHow is international shipping charged?+

Superbuy says the deposit estimate depends on destination, selected line and estimated parcel data. The logistics provider's verified size and weight determine the final charge; overpayment is refunded or an underpayment may be collected after reconciliation.

05 / FAQIs ordinary purchasing always fee-free?+

Superbuy currently lists standard purchasing from Taobao, Tmall, JD.com and 1688 as free of purchasing service fees. Second-hand, unlisted and special purchasing services can use flat or percentage fees, so check the live fee structure for the exact source.

06 / FAQDoes 90-day free storage mean a 90-day return window?+

No. Warehouse storage and seller after-sales are separate clocks. Superbuy's current fee page says storage charges begin after the free period and gives a maximum storage policy; return eligibility depends on the order, seller, item type and live after-sales status.

07 / FAQCan every warehouse item use every shipping line?+

No. Superbuy says some items are restricted to certain delivery methods. Route eligibility depends on destination, item classification and current carrier rules, so confirm batteries, liquids, powders, magnets and other sensitive goods before buying when the route is critical.

08 / FAQIs this the official Superbuy website?+

No. This is an independent informational product directory and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Superbuy.
