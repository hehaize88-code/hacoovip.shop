Editorial policy

Our standard is simple: distinguish verified platform facts, dated catalog observations and practical judgment.

Source hierarchy

Platform operations are checked first against Superbuy's official User Guidance, Help Center and fee pages. Product names, source prices and target URLs are checked against the matching live catalog page.

Dating and corrections

Articles show a visible update date, Article schema uses the same dateModified, and the sitemap uses the same lastmod. Material corrections are reviewed and dated rather than silently presented as timeless facts.

Reviews and images

We do not invent customer reviews, delivery results or QC outcomes. Customer-experience articles must identify their source and separate reported experience from verified policy. Product images are locally cached from the linked catalog record to reduce broken-image risk.
