Superbuy Parcel Forwarding Without a Tracking Number

UPDATED 2026-11-037 min read

Bought an item yourself? See what to prepare before dispatch, which tracking number to add and how to identify a parcel that has reached the warehouse.

Can you start before the seller sends a tracking number?

Yes, you can prepare the forwarding details before the seller dispatches. Superbuy’s forwarding guide separates the description of the goods and their review from the later step of entering the domestic carrier and tracking number. If the form open in your account requires a courier number to submit, keep the details ready and return when the seller provides it. Leave an unknown number blank wherever the form permits. An order reference copied into the tracking box can prevent the warehouse from matching your carton when it arrives.

First check who placed the purchase. This guide follows the path of someone who paid the seller directly and wants Superbuy to receive the parcel in mainland China before sending it abroad. When Superbuy’s shopping agent bought the goods for you, use the existing order in your account. Creating an additional forwarding request for the same goods leaves two records describing a single delivery and does nothing to move the seller’s parcel.

Keep the seller’s order number with the purchase details. It helps the seller find your transaction, confirm which version you selected and check whether all items have been packed. In a second field, enter the Chinese domestic courier and tracking number once available. That pair follows the physical parcel between the seller and the warehouse. If you later create an international parcel from your stored items, put its reference in a third field. It follows a different shipment and may contain goods from several original orders.

Prepare the goods and address while you wait

Collect the product link, a clear description, the number of units, the selected colour and size, and any details that affect which routes may accept the goods. A record that says “one blue cotton jacket, size M” is easier to reconcile than one that says “clothes”. If the item includes an electronic accessory or another unusual component, describe it accurately and check eligibility. The fact that a seller can deliver an item within China does not establish that a suitable onward route is available.

Open the forwarding service in your own Superbuy account and copy its current receiving details for the chosen warehouse. Include the identifying information supplied for your account and preserve the address layout the seller needs. Compare the seller’s delivery confirmation with that address before dispatch. A warehouse address from a forum post or an old shopping screenshot can look familiar while missing the detail that connects the package to its recipient. Keep a copy of what you actually provided in case the seller needs to correct a label.

When the seller says the parcel has shipped

Open the seller’s logistics details and copy both the carrier name and the domestic tracking number. Check the beginning and end of the number against the original label, especially if you first received it in a cropped image or chat message. A notice that a label has been created may appear before the carrier records a collection. You can use the available reference to prepare your forwarding record and then check the carrier’s first scan separately.

Return to the relevant forwarding record and complete the logistics details where the service requests them. Superbuy’s guide explains that the logistics information can be checked or modified after entry. Review the stored value against the seller’s record once more. If the seller cancels that label or changes couriers, update the affected parcel with the replacement information. Keeping the obsolete number as your only reference could send both you and support looking for a carton that never travelled under that label.

Handle a split delivery by the labels on the cartons

For example, you buy a sweatshirt and two T-shirts in one checkout. Your confirmation page shows one order number. The seller might send all three in one carton, or the sweatshirt separately from the shirts. Until the seller confirms how many packages were handed to the courier, one paid order is evidence only of the purchase. Ask for the number of cartons and the domestic tracking number for each. Record the garment assigned to each carton if the seller can identify it.

When a seller splits an order, map each physical carton to its own domestic tracking number. Superbuy’s mobile forwarding instructions also distinguish separate pieces in a multi-piece consignment. Ask for the individual package numbers if the seller supplies one master reference for several cartons. Follow the current form’s instructions for registering corresponding parcels. Do not put the same master number on several records and assume the warehouse can infer which contents belong to which box.

Now reverse the example. Two orders might travel together under a single label. List what is actually inside that parcel and keep the two seller references with it. If the account’s form or an existing request cannot express that combination clearly, ask support how to associate the items before creating overlapping records. A simple note with one line per carton, its number and its expected contents will show whether a missing T-shirt is truly missing or belongs to a second package still in transit.

If the courier shows delivery but the item is missing

Compare three pieces of evidence separately. The domestic carrier can report a delivery scan. Your forwarding record can show which parcel you told Superbuy to expect. The warehouse inventory can show whether the item was received, identified and entered under your account. A delivery scan by itself does not show that these steps have all finished. Check the exact delivery location, the latest scan time and the number stored on your forwarding request before deciding who can help.

If the carrier still has the goods or the sender used a different address, start with the seller and the domestic courier. If the delivery evidence matches your warehouse but there is no corresponding inventory entry, ask Superbuy to help match the parcel. Give support the forwarding reference, courier, tracking number, relevant delivery scan, expected contents and the identifying details used on the label. If there were two packages, say which one is missing and which one has already appeared. That detail can save another round of questions.

A useful enquiry fits into a few sentences. Say what the seller shipped, when the carrier last scanned it and which warehouse record you expected to see. Then ask one question, such as whether the warehouse can match that specific tracking number to your forwarding request. You can attach the relevant label or tracking image privately and crop unrelated order details. Keep the reply with that carton’s record so a later international enquiry starts with the right reference.

Check the stored goods before starting an international parcel

Once the goods appear in My Warehouse, compare the item description, quantity and any available photos with the purchase record you kept. Review what the receipt or inspection service actually covers. If the size marking is hidden or the quantity is unclear, request the specific check you need before choosing onward delivery. An inventory entry is useful evidence that the item was matched. It cannot resolve a dispute about the seller’s advertised materials or a detail that was never photographed.

Choose the items you want to ship and review the available routes using the actual destination, contents and parcel measurements. A missing inbound package need not delay a different item indefinitely, but sending the available items first can change packing costs and the eventual freight total. Make that choice with both groups recorded. After international dispatch, follow the parcel in My Parcel and use the international reference for questions about that leg. Keep the domestic numbers with your original purchase records in case you later need to explain what went into the shipment.
