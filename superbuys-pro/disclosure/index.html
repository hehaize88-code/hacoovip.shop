Partner and link disclosure

The site's Superbuy-focused editorial content and its product destination are not the same service.

The relationship

Superbuys.pro is not an official Superbuy website. Product, category and search links may open cnfanshp.com, a separate partner catalog that can present purchasing-platform choices.

Possible commercial benefit

The site owner may benefit when visitors use partner links. That possibility does not change the standard for describing Superbuy operations, which must be supported by official sources.

Make an informed click

Buttons that leave the site state the destination. Opening a partner page does not mean Superbuy endorses this site, the catalog, a seller or a product. Check the final destination before entering information or placing an order.
