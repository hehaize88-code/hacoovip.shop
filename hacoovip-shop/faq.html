Is this the official Hacoo website?

No. Hacoo VIP is an independent guide. Start at the official Hacoo website for its app and account information.

Can I place an order here?

This site does not sell, take payments or ship products. Catalog buttons lead to a separate external website. Check the merchant and terms at that destination.

What does this site search?

Search opens an independent external product catalog. It does not search the official Hacoo app or access your account.

Who can help with an existing order?

Use the account and support process of the service where you purchased. Keep the order ID and relevant evidence. This site cannot change orders or approve refunds.
