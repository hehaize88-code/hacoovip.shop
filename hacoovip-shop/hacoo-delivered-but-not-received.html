Hacoo Says Delivered but No Parcel? What to Check Before Contacting Support

A delivery scan is the beginning of the search. Check the location systematically, ask the right people and preserve a carrier record before opening a package-not-received investigation.

Quick answer: search the mailbox, doorway, garage, garden and approved safe place; ask family, household members and neighbors; verify the address and delivery photo; then contact the named carrier. If the Hacoo parcel is still missing, the official help page says to open the order, choose Refund/Exchange and select “I want to refund (Package not received).”

A Hacoo order marked delivered but not received can have several explanations: the parcel was placed somewhere unexpected, accepted by another person, scanned before the final drop, delivered to the wrong location or lost after the scan. Do not choose an explanation before checking the evidence. A short, structured search often solves a misplaced delivery faster; if it does not, the same record supports a carrier trace and Hacoo investigation.

Hacoo’s official EU help article gives two first checks. It tells users to look around the mailbox or drop-off location—including areas near the house, garage or lawn—and to ask family, neighbors or household members whether they accepted the parcel. If it remains missing, the article directs users to the order-page Refund/Exchange function and the package-not-received refund route.

First, confirm that the delivered scan belongs to your parcel

Open the exact order rather than relying on a push notification. Match the tracking number, product shipment, destination town or postcode and delivery date. Multi-item Hacoo orders can be divided, and Hacoo’s public shipping page says available products may ship separately from pre-ordered or back-ordered items. One delivered parcel does not necessarily complete every product line.

Save the full event before it changes. Record the carrier name, tracking number, delivered timestamp, location description, recipient name or initials, and any proof-of-delivery photo. If the event is visible only in Hacoo, check the named carrier’s own tracking page. If the carrier has a separate local number, save both numbers together.

Search the property in a fixed order

  1. Start at the named delivery point. Check the mailbox, front door, reception desk, parcel locker or location stated in the carrier event.
  2. Inspect nearby safe places. Look behind bins, gates, planters, side doors, garage areas and weather-protected corners.
  3. Check every household member. Ask whether anybody accepted, moved or stored the parcel.
  4. Ask immediate neighbors or building staff. Provide the date and carrier, but do not share unnecessary order or payment details.
  5. Review available footage. Doorbell, entrance or reception records may show whether a courier arrived at the stated time.
  6. Photograph the search context. If the delivery photo points to the wrong doorway or damaged communal area, preserve a comparison image.

Do not post the carrier photograph publicly without redacting addresses, faces, access codes and tracking numbers. The unredacted version may be useful for support, while the redacted copy is safer for public discussion.

Contact the carrier with a precise request

If the parcel is not at the property, contact the carrier shown on the tracking record. Ask it to confirm the delivery location, proof of delivery and whether a driver trace or missing-parcel investigation can be opened. Avoid saying only “I did not get it.” State the tracking number, scan time, address match and the checks already completed.

A useful request is: “Tracking XX123 was marked delivered at 14:18 on September 2. I checked the address, safe places, household members, neighbors and reception. The delivery photo does not match my entrance. Please confirm the scan location and open a driver trace.” Save the conversation date, case number, agent response and any written outcome.

Hacoo’s broader public shipping page says users should report a delivered-but-missing parcel to the shipping carrier using the information on the tracking page, and contact support with further questions. The EU help page additionally provides an in-order package-not-received route. Following both steps creates a carrier record and a platform record.

Open the Hacoo package-not-received route

After the physical search and carrier check, return to the matching Hacoo order. The current official instruction is Refund/Exchange > “I want to refund (Package not received).” Follow the prompts shown for your order; interface wording and regional requirements can change. Do not submit a different reason merely because it appears faster.

Build the investigation file

  • Order number and the products assigned to this parcel
  • Hacoo and local carrier tracking numbers
  • Delivered timestamp and full event wording
  • Delivery photo or signature record
  • Confirmation that the order address is correct
  • Short list of locations and people checked
  • Carrier case number and written response
  • Redacted comparison photo if proof shows the wrong location

Keep the description factual: “The carrier marked this parcel delivered, but it was not received at the order address. I checked the mailbox, safe places, household members, two adjacent neighbors and building reception. Carrier case 4567 is open, and the delivery photo does not match the entrance.” This is easier to investigate than speculation about theft or fraud.

What if the address on the order is wrong?

An incorrect address changes the case. Hacoo’s public shipping page says buyers are responsible for checking the shipping address and should contact support immediately when they notice an error. Its return policy lists wrong address, no consignee and invalid contact information among customer-related reasons that can affect a returned parcel and reshipment outcome.

Tell support exactly when you noticed the error and whether the parcel was already shipped. Do not claim misdelivery to the correct address if the order record shows something else. For future orders, verify street, building, unit, postcode, country and phone number before payment; see our Hacoo address-change guide.

What if the parcel appears later?

If the parcel is found by a neighbor, building staff or carrier after you opened a case, update the carrier and Hacoo promptly. Keep a photo of the parcel condition and the actual receipt time. If it arrived damaged or opened, document the outer package before opening and use the correct after-sales reason rather than continuing a non-receipt claim.

Avoid actions that weaken the claim

Do not publish your full tracking number, accuse a named person without evidence, file contradictory reasons, edit proof-of-delivery images or discard case numbers. Do not use an unfamiliar “parcel recovery” service that requests a fee, password or card details.

Also avoid refusing a later delivery without checking the current return instructions. Hacoo’s published policy says customer refusal, failure to collect and some customer-caused returns can be treated differently. If the parcel arrives after a refund request begins, report the change and follow the case-specific instruction.

Frequently asked questions

How long should I wait after a delivered scan?

Hacoo’s official page does not publish a universal waiting period. Complete the local search immediately, check the carrier proof and open a trace when the parcel is not found. Follow the deadlines displayed in your order.

Can Hacoo refund a package marked delivered?

The official help page provides a package-not-received route under Refund/Exchange. The result depends on the investigation and evidence; opening the route does not guarantee a particular outcome.

What evidence is best for a wrong-address delivery?

Preserve the order address, carrier event, delivery photo, location information, building comparison and carrier case result. Keep private details inside the support case.

What if a neighbor accepted the parcel?

Confirm the parcel number and condition, then close or update any investigation already opened. Photograph damage or tampering before opening when relevant.

Bottom line

If Hacoo says delivered but no parcel is present, search first, verify second and report third. The official workflow begins with the delivery area and people nearby, then moves to the order-page package-not-received route. Adding carrier proof, a precise timeline and a concise list of completed checks gives the investigation something concrete to test.

For a parcel that has not reached the delivered stage, use the tracking-not-updating checklist. For understanding the earlier stages, read Hacoo order status explained.
