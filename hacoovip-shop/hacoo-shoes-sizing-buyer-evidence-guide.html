Hacoo Shoes: Size Charts, Foot Measurements and Link Checks

Check a Hacoo shoe link, compare foot measurements with the exact listing size chart and inspect the evidence before choosing an option.

Hacoo VIP · Updated 2026-11-02

Verify the shoe before choosing a size

A shared shoe photo is a lead, not a sizing reference. Open the product page and match the model, identifier, color and option to the saved recommendation. Our category page opens an independent research catalog, separate from the official Hacoo app. Use the size chart belonging to the destination you actually intend to order from.

Similar images can appear on different listings. If the model or chart changes, redo the sizing check instead of carrying over a recommendation from another row.

Measure both feet consistently

  1. Wear the type of socks you expect to use with the shoes.
  2. Stand on a flat surface and record each foot's heel-to-longest-toe length in centimeters or millimeters.
  3. Record the measurement method and use the longer foot when comparing the chart.
  4. Check width, fit notes and the chart's stated measurement type.
  5. Ask for clarification when the listing does not say whether a value is foot length, insole length or another measurement.

Do not automatically add a fixed allowance or convert EU, UK and US sizes using a generic table. Models differ, and a seller's chart may use a different convention. A removable insole measurement can be useful evidence, but it is not automatically equivalent to usable internal space or recommended foot length.

Read the chart as a set of claims

Size systemDoes the option use EU, UK, US or a listing-specific label?
LengthWhat is being measured, and in which units?
Width and shapeAre fit notes available for the toe box and midfoot?
Model and optionDoes the chart apply to this exact model and selected variation?
Review evidenceDoes the reviewer give foot measurements and the purchased size?

Inspect photographs for specific questions

Look for both shoes from the front, side, heel and sole. Check visible symmetry, material differences, seams and the size label. A photograph can show a visible inconsistency; it cannot establish comfort, durability, authenticity or an accurate internal measurement by itself.

Give more weight to a review explaining the reviewer's measurements and use than to an isolated “true to size” label. Keep unresolved fit questions visible in your notes rather than treating missing information as a positive result.

Before checkout and after arrival

Save the chosen option, chart, relevant review and return instructions. Confirm that the displayed price belongs to that option. When the shoes arrive, compare the labels and visible construction with the order record, then assess fit according to the applicable return conditions before outdoor wear.

If the wrong size or item arrives, photograph the label and the complete item and use the return evidence guide. For uncertainty before purchase, use the product-page checklist to identify what still needs an answer.
