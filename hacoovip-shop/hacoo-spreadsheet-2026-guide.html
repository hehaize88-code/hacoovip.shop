Hacoo Spreadsheet: Product Links, Categories and Checks

Use a Hacoo spreadsheet to compare product links, item IDs, sizes and prices. Learn what to record and how to spot outdated rows.

What a Hacoo spreadsheet can help you do

A spreadsheet organizes product leads. Its useful job is to connect an item description to a specific current destination and preserve the details needed to compare it. It cannot guarantee quality, authenticity, availability or delivery. Start with a product category or the external research catalog, then verify each item yourself.

This catalog is separate from the official Hacoo app. Keep the source name next to every identifier so you do not expect a code from one service to work in another.

The columns worth keeping

ColumnRecordWhy it matters
Source and direct URLCatalog name and product routeA homepage does not identify an item.
Item and optionID, model, color, size, set contentsSimilar pictures may describe different options.
MeasurementsUnits, relevant dimensions, source of measurementA size label alone is not a fit check.
PriceSelected-option price, original currency, dateA minimum price may belong to another option.
EvidenceDetail photos, review context, missing informationSeparates what is visible from assumptions.
StatusCurrent, needs review, unavailablePrevents stale links from appearing verified.

Use one category at a time

For shoes, compare foot measurements with the listing's size chart and check the exact model. For clothing, record garment dimensions, fabric composition when stated and the intended fit. For bags, compare width, height, depth, strap dimensions and closure. See the shoe-size guide and bag checklist.

Choose a small shortlist with comparable requirements. Ten poorly documented links are less useful than three records with matching identifiers, measurements and current options.

Example: a saved hoodie link opens a different item

Suppose a saved row says “black zip hoodie, medium.” The destination now opens a pullover with no medium option. Mark that row as changed; do not update only the picture and call it verified. Save a replacement under its own identifier and compare chest width, garment length and sleeve length before deciding whether it is an alternative.

Check freshness and cost separately

Reopen the direct product route when you are ready to order. Check the selected option, currency and destination-specific checkout total. Keep delivery and any other stated charges separate from the item price. Do not add agent or warehouse fees unless you are actually using a separate service that charges them; they are not a universal Hacoo workflow.

A checked date describes your own check, not a promise of daily updates. Preserve broken rows as history and use the link-recovery guide when a route changes. Before treating a new URL as the same item, compare its identifier and specifications.
