Hacoo Returns and Refunds: Request, Evidence and Timing

Prepare a Hacoo return or refund request, check the applicable deadline and keep the evidence needed to follow up on approval and payment.

Hacoo VIP · Updated 2026-11-02

On this page
  • Start with your order's return instructions
  • Build a concise evidence record
  • Send one clear request
  • Understand timing without treating it as a guarantee
  • Exceptions and next steps

Start with your order's return instructions

Open the order in the service where you purchased and save the current return terms before the deadline. Hacoo's public refund policy reviewed on 2 November 2026 describes returns for most products within 15 days of receipt, with category exceptions. Check that the policy applies to your order and destination; do not assume every item qualifies.

Eligibility, request approval, return delivery and money arriving are different stages. Keep a separate date for each. This independent guide cannot approve a request or access your account.

Build a concise evidence record

IssueEvidence to preserve
Wrong item or optionOrder selection, received item's label and full-item photo.
Visible damage or defectClose-up, wider context, packaging and delivery date.
Missing piecesOrdered set contents, packing slip and all received items.
Fit or description mismatchListing measurements and comparable measurements of the received item.
Parcel not receivedTracking history, carrier response and delivery checks.

Keep originals and submit the few images that explain the problem clearly. Do not edit evidence to exaggerate a fault. Avoid including unrelated personal details.

Send one clear request

  1. Identify the order and affected item or quantity.
  2. State what was expected and what arrived.
  3. Attach the relevant evidence and explain the outcome you are requesting.
  4. Save the submission time, case reference and response.
  5. Follow the approved return address and method if a return is required.

Do not ship to an address copied from an old article or refuse a parcel solely to trigger a refund. Obtain instructions for your case. Confirm who pays return postage and whether a supplied label is required before spending money.

Understand timing without treating it as a guarantee

The public policy lists a review period that can extend around holidays or promotions, and different refund-crediting periods for cards and PayPal. Your approval notice and payment provider are the appropriate references for a specific refund. Save the approval date, method and any transaction reference; an approved request is not proof that funds have reached the original account.

If the stated period passes, follow up in the existing case with those details. Check the original payment method and distinguish a pending authorization, a completed debit and a posted refund.

Exceptions and next steps

The public policy lists restrictions for opened or activated electronics, sealed appliances and some personal-care items. Wrong-item or quality-problem cases may be treated differently. Preserve packaging and labels while the case is unresolved and check the instructions before opening a restricted item.

Use refund pending for payment follow-up, rejected requests for a reason-based review, and return shipping costs before posting a parcel. The practical evidence steps here are editorial guidance; the linked policy and your order instructions determine the applicable process.
