Hacoo Return Shipping Costs: Who Pays and What to Check

A return approval does not automatically tell you who pays the postage. Separate the item refund from the cost of sending it back before you buy a label.

Start here: ask for the approved return reason, exact destination, accepted shipping method and written cost decision. A carrier quote tells you what sending costs; the case decision tells you what may be reimbursed. Keep both records.

Searching for “Hacoo return shipping cost” usually means that a return has become a financial decision. A product may qualify for a return, yet the postage could make a small refund less useful. Alternatively, a damaged or incorrect item may have an approved cost arrangement that is easy to miss in a long support conversation. The practical task is to establish what the particular approval includes.

What the published fee guidance actually covers

Hacoo’s public return-fee help page distinguishes customer-reason returns from cases accepted as Hacoo’s responsibility. It assigns shipping and customs costs to the customer in the former case, and describes shipping compensation of up to US$30 in the latter. It also says the customer pays postage first and that payment on delivery is not accepted. This is public guidance, not confirmation that your order qualifies for compensation.

Another public policy page uses “free return” language. Read that alongside the detailed fee rules and the instructions attached to your order. If the wording conflicts, ask support to resolve the difference before shipping. Do not treat a headline, an old screenshot or somebody else’s reimbursement as a prepaid label for your parcel.

Separate the four amounts in a return

AmountWhere to verify itWhat to ask
Item refundWritten case decisionWhich items and quantities are approved?
Original delivery chargeOrder invoice and decisionIs any part included in the refund?
Return postageCarrier quote and shipping receiptWho pays first, and is reimbursement approved?
Other return chargesCarrier terms and case instructionsAre surcharges or customs-related costs included?

Write each amount on a separate line. “Refund approved” is too vague when it could refer only to the product price. If two products from one order have different decisions, keep separate item amounts even if support authorizes one return parcel. That makes it possible to reconcile a partial payment later without reopening the entire order.

Confirm the reason without changing the facts

Describe what happened in ordinary language before selecting a category. An item you no longer want, a correctly supplied size that does not fit, an incorrect size label, and a damaged product are different situations. Use the reason that matches the order and the evidence. A more favorable category is not useful if the photographs contradict it.

For a wrong-item claim, compare the ordered variant with the label and the received product. For a defect, show the specific problem at a useful scale. For a preference return, keep the explanation straightforward. Ask support to name the accepted reason in its decision so that the postage arrangement is connected to the same facts.

Get a usable quote after the address is confirmed

Do not price the return using the address printed on the delivery parcel. A dispatch address, carrier depot or consolidation label may serve a different purpose from the approved return destination. Copy the address from the active case, including any reference, postcode and recipient details. If something is missing, clarify it rather than completing the address from a search result.

Weigh and measure the parcel in its final packaging. Ask the carrier which service accepts that destination and the contents. Record the tracking arrangement, estimated delivery range and any optional charges separately. A low headline quote is not a useful comparison if it excludes the parcel dimensions or does not provide the evidence requested in the case.

If support specifies a dispatch or upload deadline, compare that deadline with the practical shipping options before paying. Keep a screenshot of the instruction. Where there is a choice of service, ask whether the proposed option meets the return requirements; do not assume the fastest service receives a higher reimbursement.

Use a simple cost comparison

Planning estimate: confirmed item refund, plus separately approved fee reimbursements, minus the costs you must pay. Keep unconfirmed reimbursement outside the calculation until it is approved.

For an illustrative example, suppose an item refund is €40 and a carrier quotes €14 for the return. With no postage reimbursement confirmed, the planning estimate is €26 before any other applicable costs. These are invented arithmetic inputs, not Hacoo prices, a carrier quote or a customer result. Their purpose is to show why the postage decision matters.

Make a second line for a proposed reimbursement, but label it unconfirmed until the case accepts it. Do not silently convert an advertised maximum into money you expect to receive. A limit is different from an approved amount, and currency conversion can make a rough comparison differ from the eventual payment.

Ask for the missing decision in one message

A useful support message contains the order reference, approved item, accepted reason, destination supplied and proposed carrier service. Then ask the unresolved cost question directly. Keep the conversation in the order’s support route so the response can be matched to the approval.

Adaptable message: “My return for [item] in [order] is approved for [reason]. The quoted postage to the supplied address is [amount/currency] using [service]. Please confirm whether this cost is reimbursable, the approved limit, the evidence you require and where any reimbursement will appear.”

If the response addresses the item refund but ignores postage, ask that narrower question again. Avoid buying another label simply because the first reply was unclear. If the return deadline is close, include that date and request written instructions about how to proceed while the fee question is reviewed.

Keep evidence at the moment of dispatch

  1. Record the contents. Photograph the authorized items, identifying labels and included accessories before sealing the parcel.
  2. Record the package. Keep a clear photograph of the packed parcel and completed address label.
  3. Keep the receipt. Save the amount, currency, service name, acceptance date and tracking reference.
  4. Upload as instructed. Attach the shipping evidence to the same case and retain confirmation that the upload succeeded.

A purchased label and a parcel accepted by the carrier are different records. Keep the acceptance receipt when one is available. If the carrier corrects a label or charges an adjustment, retain both versions with a short explanation. The final expense should be understandable without requiring a reviewer to reconstruct it from unrelated screenshots.

Reconcile postage separately when the refund arrives

When a payment or balance credit appears, match its amount and description to the approval. Check whether it covers the item, the shipping reimbursement or both. A missing postage amount needs a focused follow-up with the written cost decision and receipt; it does not automatically mean that the item refund failed.

If support cannot identify a payment, ask for its destination and reference before checking a bank or wallet. Use the Hacoo refund pending guide to separate case review, refund processing and actual credit. Avoid assuming that every refund travels through the same payment route.

Resolve uncertainty before creating a second expense

Keep the original parcel and current return case together as one record. If the package is rejected, delayed or sent back by a carrier, ask for updated instructions before resending it. A second journey can create a second fee without resolving the first problem. Do not interpret a carrier return as a new Hacoo approval.

For the wider eligibility and evidence process, continue with Hacoo return policy and refund methods. If the application itself has been refused, use the return request rejected checklist before arranging postage. The useful endpoint is a written decision that connects one item, one reason, one destination and a clearly stated cost arrangement.
