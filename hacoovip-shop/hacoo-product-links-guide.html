Hacoo Links: Find Products and Fix Broken Links

Find Hacoo product links, check their destination and recover an old link without confusing a similar item with the original.

Start with the destination, then the product

A Hacoo link may lead to a community post, an app screen, a product page or an independent catalog. Identify which one you have before searching for a replacement. Hacoo VIP is an independent guide: our catalog buttons open an external research catalog, not your Hacoo account or an official Hacoo checkout.

For official access, start at Hacoo's website and follow its app link. To browse the external research catalog offered here, use categories. Keep these two routes separate: an item number from one service may have no meaning in another.

A practical link check

  1. Save the original URL, the item description and the date you received it.
  2. Open the link and inspect the final destination after redirects. A homepage is not a recovered product page.
  3. Compare the product identifier, model, photographs and selected color or size with your original record.
  4. Check availability and the currency on the destination itself. A screenshot can preserve an old price or a sold-out option.
  5. Keep the new URL only when the important fields match. Label a similar item as an alternative.

What to do when a link does not work

Visible resultNext useful check
Opens the homepageReturn to the original post and look for a current direct link or product identifier.
Opens another productStop treating it as the old recommendation. Compare identifiers and options.
App prompt appearsUse the app route linked by the official website; then retry the original link.
Product loads but your size is unavailableThe route works. Record the unavailable option and compare alternatives separately.
Error or blank screenTry a normal page on the same service to distinguish a connection problem from one missing listing.

Recover an old link from a screenshot

Extract concrete clues: the visible product ID, a distinctive construction detail, dimensions, option label and source name. Search the original service first. If you use an image search, treat the results as candidates. Two shoes can share a cover image while differing in model, size chart or seller.

For example, a saved bag shows a zip closure and a 24 cm width. A new candidate with a flap closure and no measurements is an alternative, even if the color matches. Record which fields match and which remain unknown; do not silently replace the saved link.

Keep a small link log

Use columns for source, original URL, product ID, current URL, option, date checked and outcome. Useful outcomes are matching page, needs review and unavailable. Preserve the original when a route changes, so you can explain why a recommendation changed. A working link alone cannot establish quality, authenticity or delivery eligibility.

If all direct routes fail, ask the original publisher for the current item reference. For account or app problems, use official support. Nobody needs your password or payment details to help identify a broken public link.
