Hacoo Shipping Help: Estimates and Tracking

Start with your order estimate and the latest shipment event.

Hacoo VIP · Updated 2026-11-02

On this page
  • Use the details on your order
  • Choose the guide that matches the status

Use the details on your order

Hacoo VIP does not ship products or have access to your orders. Check the delivery estimate, actual destination and carrier shown in the service where you purchased. An order reference and a parcel tracking number may be different.

Choose the guide that matches the status

  • Delivery time and tracking: preparation, dispatch and final delivery.
  • Tracking not updating: record the last event and compare it with the estimate.
  • Delivered but not received: check delivery locations and contact the carrier promptly.
  • Returned to sender: ask about the reason and available next steps.

Public help pages provide general estimates, not an individual arrival guarantee. Save the relevant tracking event, order estimate and support response before following up. If an independent catalog sends you to another merchant, use that merchant’s order instructions.
