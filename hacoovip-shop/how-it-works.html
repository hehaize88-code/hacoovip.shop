How to Shop on Hacoo: A First-Order Checklist

Choose the correct Hacoo app or independent catalog, verify the product and save delivery and return terms before your first order.

Hacoo VIP · Updated 2026-11-02

On this page
  • Choose the service you intend to use
  • Move from discovery to a verified item
  • Use a guide for the decision in front of you
  • After ordering

Choose the service you intend to use

For official Hacoo access, start with the official website and its current app link. Hacoo VIP provides independent guides and links to a separate product research catalog. We do not hold your account or process purchases. See the app and website guide if you are unsure which route you have opened.

Move from discovery to a verified item

  1. Find a product through a category, current link or descriptive search.
  2. Confirm the identifier, selected color or size and included pieces.
  3. Read the measurements and detail photos for that exact listing.
  4. Check who takes payment and where the order will appear.
  5. Review the checkout total, currency, actual destination and delivery estimate.
  6. Save the return conditions and contact route together with the confirmation.

Use a guide for the decision in front of you

Use product links when a shared route is broken, the spreadsheet guide to compare candidates, and shoe measurements before choosing a footwear option. Each guide preserves the distinction between a product lead and a verified match.

After ordering

Keep the order ID separate from shipment references. Follow the estimate and tracking provided for your purchase; a general shipping article cannot predict an individual parcel's arrival. If something is wrong, preserve the item, packaging and relevant screenshots and use the order's support process promptly.

The delivery guide explains tracking stages. The return and refund guide helps you prepare a clear case. An external guide cannot approve a refund or change the address on an existing order.
