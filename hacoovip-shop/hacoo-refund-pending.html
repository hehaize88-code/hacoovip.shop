Hacoo Refund Pending: Card, PayPal and Payment-Channel Timelines

“Refund pending” can describe review, approval, processing or bank credit. Identify the exact stage before deciding that the money is late.

Quick answer: Hacoo’s currently published policy says refund applications are reviewed within 24 hours, with possible extensions during holidays or promotions. After processing, it displays 8–15 working days for credit cards and 5–7 working days for PayPal. Count from the refund-processing event—not from the first complaint—and verify the original payment account.

A Hacoo refund can feel late when two different clocks are mixed together. The first clock covers the platform’s decision: was the return, cancellation, missing parcel or product problem approved? The second clock starts when an approved refund is actually sent toward the original payment channel. A third delay may appear inside the bank or payment provider before the credit becomes visible.

The current public Return & Refund Policy lists a 24-hour review period and says holiday or promotion periods may extend it. The same table lists 8–15 working days for credit-card refunds and 5–7 working days for PayPal. Hacoo’s EU after-sales help result separately says crediting time depends on the original payment channel and advises contacting online service if the amount does not arrive within the stated limit. These are published ranges, not a guarantee for every case or country.

Identify which refund stage is pending

Request submittedYou sent the application, but there may be no decision yet. Save the submission date and requested amount.
Under reviewHacoo may be checking eligibility, evidence, return tracking or parcel investigation details.
Approved/processedA decision exists and the refund may have been initiated. Record the processing date, amount, currency and route.
CreditedThe original payment account shows the money. Match the amount and transaction reference to the order.

Do not start the card or PayPal countdown while the request is still under review. Look for wording that confirms both approval and processing. An accepted return label, a customer-service promise or a cancelled order does not always show the exact date the payment instruction was issued.

“Working days” generally excludes weekends and may exclude bank holidays. Do not convert 15 working days into 15 calendar days. Also check whether the payment was a temporary authorization rather than a completed charge. A released authorization can disappear from pending activity instead of appearing as a separate refund credit.

Build a refund timeline before following up

  1. Find the original charge. Record the date, amount, currency, payment channel and transaction reference.
  2. Find the refund request. Save the case number, reason, submitted evidence and requested amount.
  3. Find the decision. Distinguish approved, partially approved, rejected or waiting for return evidence.
  4. Find the processing date. Look for the message that indicates the refund was issued or sent.
  5. Count working days. Use the applicable channel range and exclude non-working days.
  6. Check the correct account. Review the same card or PayPal account used for payment, including pending and completed activity.
  7. Follow up with one record. Ask Hacoo for the refund reference or ask the payment provider to trace that reference.

Why the refunded amount may look different

A refund should be matched by transaction, not only by the number shown in your shopping currency. A card issuer may display the credit in the card’s settlement currency, and exchange-rate movement can make the displayed converted amount differ from the original local-currency view. A partial approval can also produce a smaller credit than the order total.

Separate the item price, original shipping, return shipping and any other amount shown in the case. Hacoo’s policy says ordinary preference returns can make the customer responsible for return shipping, while damaged, defective or incorrect-item cases can be handled differently. Use the written case result to determine what was approved; do not assume every fee is included.

Card refund pending: what to check

Verify that you are viewing the original card account, including a replaced card connected to the same issuer. Search activity around both the purchase and processing dates. Some issuers display the refund as a separate credit; others link it to the original transaction. If the card was replaced, expired or closed, ask the issuer how incoming merchant refunds are routed.

After the published period has passed, ask Hacoo for the refund processing reference, date, amount and currency. Then give that information to the card issuer. Avoid opening a payment dispute while a trace is already in progress unless your provider tells you to do so or a formal deadline requires action; contradictory processes can make the record harder to follow.

PayPal refund pending: what to check

Open the original PayPal transaction and look for a linked refund, not only the wallet balance. Depending on how the payment was funded, PayPal may route the money back to a card or bank rather than leave it as balance. Record the PayPal transaction ID and the refund status shown in the activity detail.

If the Hacoo processing date is outside the published 5–7 working-day range, request the refund reference and compare it with PayPal activity. Ask which funding source should receive the money. Do not send your password, one-time code or full card number to anyone offering to “release” the refund.

When the refund request is still under review

A review can remain open because evidence is incomplete, a returned parcel has not been confirmed, a carrier investigation is pending or the request falls in a restricted category. Open the case and look for a request to upload photographs, tracking, shipping receipt, product labels or an explanation of the problem. Reply to that requirement directly.

For a wrong, damaged or missing item, use the evidence categories in our Hacoo return and refund evidence guide. For a parcel marked delivered but missing, preserve the carrier record and use the dedicated package-not-received checklist.

Write a refund follow-up that can be traced

Include these details

  • Order number and refund case number
  • Approved amount and currency
  • Original payment channel
  • Refund-processing date and message
  • Number of working days elapsed
  • Screenshot showing the original transaction and no matching credit
  • A request for the refund reference, not a vague status update

Example: “Refund case 1934 for order ending 6620 was approved and marked processed on August 10 for €54.20 to the original Visa card. Fifteen working days have passed and the issuer cannot find the credit. Please provide the refund transaction reference, processing date and destination details.” Redact full card numbers in screenshots and leave only the minimum identifying information.

Red flags during a refund follow-up

Do not pay a separate “refund release” fee, share a one-time password, install remote-access software or move the conversation to an unverified account. Use the order’s in-app route and the payment provider’s official support channel.

Be cautious if a public commenter asks for your order number or full payment evidence. Screenshots can expose names, addresses, transaction IDs and card details. Keep unredacted material inside the official case.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Hacoo refund clock start?

The payment-channel period should be counted from the confirmed refund-processing date, not automatically from the complaint, return request or order cancellation date.

Why can I not see a separate card refund?

The issuer may link the credit to the original charge, release an authorization or route it to a replacement card account. Ask the issuer to search using the refund reference.

Does Hacoo refund shipping costs?

It depends on the approved reason and case. The current policy treats ordinary preference returns and damaged, defective or incorrect items differently. Read the amount stated in the decision.

What should I do after the published time limit?

Ask Hacoo for the processing reference, date, amount, currency and destination, then ask the original payment provider to trace that reference.

Bottom line

A Hacoo refund pending is easiest to solve when review, approval, processing and credit are recorded as separate events. Confirm the payment channel, find the processing date, count working days correctly and follow up with a transaction reference. That turns “my refund is missing” into a traceable payment question.
