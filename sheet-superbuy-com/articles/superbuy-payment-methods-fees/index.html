Payments and account balance

Superbuy Payment Methods & Fees: Cards, Wise and Balance

Compare payment options using the amount debited and CNY credited, keep product and freight budgets separate, and resolve pending payments without creating duplicate charges.

Which Superbuy payment methods should you compare?

Superbuy's shopping guide lists cards, Alipay, WeChat and bank transfers; its Help Center also links to Wise. These references identify options to investigate, not a promise that every method will appear for every account. Open your own payment or top-up screen, select the currency you intend to use, and read the available choices before comparing costs. An old screenshot cannot establish today's availability, minimum amount, verification requirements or fee for your transaction.

Start with a modest, defined purchase budget rather than funding a large speculative balance. Write down the item total, domestic delivery, selected services and a separate international-shipping allowance. Decide which payment method you can document clearly if a transfer needs tracing. Convenience matters, but a familiar logo alone does not show the total cost. Compare the exact transaction presented to you, including any charge displayed by your bank or payment provider before confirmation.

Read four numbers before you confirm

A useful comparison records four things: the currency leaving your account, the total amount leaving it, the CNY amount credited or applied to the order, and any later issuer charge. A displayed exchange rate is only one input. A payment can have a competitive headline rate and still cost more after a fixed fee. Conversely, a method with a visible fee can produce a better net result. Use the same desired CNY amount and compare quotes close together in time.

The table is a recording template, not a fee schedule. Fill it with the final payment screen and your provider's confirmation. If a fee is already included in the total debit, do not add it again. If the issuer has not yet settled the transaction, label that component provisional. Keep screenshots with the date, amount and transaction reference, while excluding full card numbers, security codes and other information that customer support does not need.

An illustrative exchange-cost comparison

Suppose two hypothetical methods would each credit CNY 700. Method A debits USD 103 in total, while Method B debits USD 105. Before any separately billed issuer charge, A costs USD 2 less for the same credit. Dividing CNY credited by the total USD debit gives approximately 6.796 for A and 6.667 for B. These are invented arithmetic inputs for comparison; they are not current Superbuy exchange rates or quotes for a real payment.

Now suppose A later attracts a separately billed USD 3 issuer charge while B has none. A's complete debit becomes USD 106, reversing the earlier comparison. This is why the cheapest-looking payment screen need not be the cheapest completed transaction. Reconcile the final statement against the credited balance before drawing a conclusion. Compare like with like: a card authorisation, a settled payment, a transfer quote and a bank account debit can represent different stages of the same transaction.

Cards, Wise and transfers: follow the actual instructions

For a card payment, check the selected currency, billing details, issuer approval step and final total. If authentication fails, read the error before retrying. A mismatch in address or an issuer restriction needs a different remedy from a temporary page error. For a Wise or bank-transfer option shown in your account, copy the current beneficiary details and required reference exactly from the official payment flow. Never reuse payment instructions from an unsolicited message or an unrelated older order.

A transfer reference can be essential for matching a payment to the intended account. Save the sender receipt, reference, currency and amount, then check whether the account records a credit. Do not infer completion merely because the sending provider accepted the instruction. If the quoted processing window passes, send support a concise reconciliation request through the official account. Include only the necessary payment evidence, and ask which stage remains unresolved instead of opening several competing requests.

Account balance is a ledger, not a shipping budget

Treat your balance as a transaction record. Note each top-up, order payment, service charge, refund and adjustment so that a changing total has an explanation. If CNY 500 is credited and an order uses CNY 320, the arithmetic remainder is CNY 180 before other entries. That example does not establish whether a particular balance can be withdrawn, whether credits have conditions or whether a promotional amount follows the same rules as money you paid.

Do not spend the whole remainder on another product until you have estimated the first parcel. Product payment and international freight are separate stages in Superbuy's fee structure. Leave room for packing choices, the measured parcel, the selected line and any confirmed adjustments. Use the shipping-cost guide to build the parcel budget, then replace estimates with the account's actual quote. A small available balance is not evidence that the international shipping bill has already been paid.

Pending, failed and duplicate-looking payments need different checks

When a payment looks pending, compare the provider's status with the order or balance history. Record the time and reference, then follow the processing guidance displayed for that method. A pending authorisation does not by itself establish that a second completed charge exists. When a payment explicitly fails, identify the stated cause and confirm whether a debit or balance credit nevertheless appeared before attempting another payment. Repeated retries make an unclear record harder to reconcile.

If two entries appear, first determine whether they are two settled transactions or a temporary authorisation alongside a settlement. Compare amounts, currencies and references rather than relying on matching dates. Send the official support team a brief timeline and ask them to identify the credited transaction. If your payment provider must investigate, use its own support process. Keep the order decision separate: do not approve another product purchase simply to use a credit whose origin is still uncertain.

A refund, a balance credit and a withdrawal are different outcomes

An order cancellation request is not proof of an approved refund, and an approved refund is not proof that money has returned to the original funding account. Check the order decision, the transaction ledger and the receiving payment account separately. Ask where the refund will go, what amount is approved, whether any charges are excluded and what processing guidance applies. Do not assume that exchange differences or third-party payment costs will be reversed automatically.

Before topping up more than needed, read the current rules for withdrawing unused paid balance. A promotional credit may have different conditions; distinguish it from your own funds. For a refund after a QC problem, keep the seller remedy and return costs in the order conversation, then reconcile the financial entries after the decision. Our returns guide explains that evidence workflow. This payment guide does not promise a fixed refund deadline, a free withdrawal or a universal return entitlement.

Save a reusable payment record

Create one record per payment with the related order or parcel number, method, currency, quoted total, confirmed debit, credited amount, reference and status. Add a short note when the payment changes from pending to credited, or when a refund or adjustment appears. This is enough to answer most reconciliation questions without a pile of unlabelled screenshots. For a later purchase, start a new record so that a top-up for one parcel is not mistaken for another order's payment.

Before confirming, check that you are on the official site, the intended account is signed in, the amount matches the purchase stage and you understand any displayed fee. After confirming, verify the matching account entry and keep the receipt until the order and parcel are resolved. The useful target is a traceable, correctly funded purchase, not a particular payment brand. Recheck the available options for each new payment because country, account, currency and provider conditions may change.
