Reader’s margin

Questions worth answering before the buy button.

A directory can shorten product discovery. It cannot guarantee authenticity, seller conduct, material performance or a future shipping price.

Open the FAQ / Help Desk →
What role does Kakobuys.pro play in an order?+

It plays no transactional role. The site is an independent publication that organises product destinations and inspection guidance; it does not accept payment, purchase goods or manage parcels.

Why is a direct product link still not enough evidence?+

A link can become outdated, a seller can change options, and the item received at a warehouse can differ from the ordered variation. Match the live listing, order record and warehouse entry before judging finish.

What is the first thing to check in QC photos?+

Confirm identity before quality: product version, colour, marked size, quantity and major options. Detailed defect inspection is wasted if the photographed item is not the ordered item.

Can a clean-looking QC set prove authenticity?+

No. Warehouse images can support observations about visible shape, printing, labels and damage. They do not independently prove authenticity, materials, internal construction or long-term performance.
