Dior Bag — QC field note

This file concentrates on bags: inspect structure, attachment points and hardware consistency.

Dior BagCase reference: 7673174018

Start by proving it is the item you ordered

Before judging finish, compare the order record with the warehouse entry: option, colour, marked size, quantity and any seller variation. A sharp photo of the wrong version is still the wrong product.

For bags , inspect the whole outline first and only then zoom into details. This avoids missing a large mismatch while studying one seam.

Three visible checkpoints

1. Handle anchoringUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.
2. Edge and stitch rhythmUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.
3. Hardware setUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.

What the frames cannot settle

Warehouse images do not reliably prove fibre content, long-term wear, internal construction, electrical function or authenticity. Those questions require seller evidence, specifications or testing beyond a photograph.
