Gallery Dept pants — QC field note

This file concentrates on pants & shorts: prioritise actual waist, rise and leg balance over tagged size.

Gallery Dept pantsCase reference: 7725931994

Start by proving it is the item you ordered

Before judging finish, compare the order record with the warehouse entry: option, colour, marked size, quantity and any seller variation. A sharp photo of the wrong version is still the wrong product.

For pants & shorts , inspect the whole outline first and only then zoom into details. This avoids missing a large mismatch while studying one seam.

Three visible checkpoints

1. Waist measurementUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.
2. Rise and inseamUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.
3. Pocket symmetryUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.

What the frames cannot settle

Warehouse images do not reliably prove fibre content, long-term wear, internal construction, electrical function or authenticity. Those questions require seller evidence, specifications or testing beyond a photograph.
