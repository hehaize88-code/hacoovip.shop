LV Shoes — QC field note

This file concentrates on shoes: read shape, pair balance and sole joins before logos.

LV ShoesCase reference: 7661965522

Start by proving it is the item you ordered

Before judging finish, compare the order record with the warehouse entry: option, colour, marked size, quantity and any seller variation. A sharp photo of the wrong version is still the wrong product.

For shoes , inspect the whole outline first and only then zoom into details. This avoids missing a large mismatch while studying one seam.

Three visible checkpoints

1. Pair silhouetteUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.
2. Midsole and outsole joinsUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.
3. Size tag against orderUse the available angles and measurements together. If the necessary view is absent, treat the conclusion as unresolved rather than positive.

What the frames cannot settle

Warehouse images do not reliably prove fibre content, long-term wear, internal construction, electrical function or authenticity. Those questions require seller evidence, specifications or testing beyond a photograph.
