About this project

A smaller claim is often a better claim.

Kakobuys.pro is an independent product-discovery and editorial project. It makes spreadsheet research easier to navigate while showing the boundary between evidence and opinion.

What we publish

The site organises product categories and practical guides for reading warehouse QC photographs. It does not operate a purchasing service, warehouse, shipping line or marketplace.

How facts are sourced

Policy notes are checked against Kakobuy’s publicly indexed About, Services and Fees, Inspection and Storage, Inspection Information, five-day guarantee, package-photo and notice pages. Every time-sensitive claim is dated. Product destinations are reviewed at URL level, product cards use the destination’s first image, and no rating or order history is invented.

What independence means

Kakobuys.pro is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kakobuy, Taobao, Weidian, 1688, Tmall or any brand named in a third-party listing. Trademarks belong to their owners.

Corrections

Product availability, shipping services and seller policies change. Outdated material should be corrected or clearly dated instead of silently presented as current fact.

Review standard

Four rules

  • Name and date time-sensitive sources
  • Date policy reviews
  • Keep shopping clicks on one reviewed destination
  • Never invent an order experience
