Front page / The desk

About this publication

A field journal built to slow down bad decisions

Kakobuys.pro is an independent editorial directory for people researching Kakobuy-related product discovery, QC photos and parcel choices.

Why it looks like a case file

Most spreadsheet sites reward speed and volume. This publication uses an evidence-led layout because the important work begins after a product link is found: matching the order, reading the available frames and checking time-sensitive conditions.

How claims enter an article

Published platform information is checked against the accessible source and given a review date. Category advice is framed as an inspection method, not as a promise that a product or seller is reliable.

Where product links lead

Catalog and product actions open matching destinations on cnfanshp.com. This site does not take payment, create orders or store customer parcels.

What independence means here

Kakobuys.pro is not an official Kakobuy property and is not endorsed by referenced marketplaces or brands. Independence is stated so readers can judge the role of every page.

Publication standard

Four desk rules

  • No invented buyer stories
  • No photo treated as authenticity proof
  • No fixed freight promise without a live quote
  • No hidden destination behind a product action
