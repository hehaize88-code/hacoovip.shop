Taobao link guide

How to Buy from Taobao with Kakobuy Without Losing the Variant Details

A source-first method for importing a Taobao link into Kakobuy, preserving seller and variant evidence, checking warehouse photos and budgeting the later parcel.

By Kakobuys.pro Research Desk12 min read1,492 wordsReviewed July 30, 2026

A Taobao link is not a product guarantee. It is a route to a seller page that can contain several colours, sizes, bundles and changing promotional prices. Kakobuy can act on the URL and instructions you submit, but it cannot know which interpretation you intended if the imported options are unclear. The practical skill is preserving enough source evidence that the warehouse result can be compared with the order.

This guide treats Taobao as the source marketplace and Kakobuy as the purchasing and forwarding interface. Kakobuy’s public pages identify Taobao among the supported purchasing-agent contexts and describe purchasing products from URLs supplied by users. The steps below focus on the information that can be checked, not on claims about a seller’s quality or authenticity.

Use one source of truthRecord the live Taobao seller, item title, selected SKU text, size chart, price and URL before importing. If Kakobuy’s translated page conflicts with the source page, stop and resolve the conflict before payment.

Open the Taobao page before you copy it

Spreadsheet links and social posts often add a convenient English label, but that label can outlive the destination. Open the Taobao page and check that it still shows the intended product family. Look for sudden changes in images, a different seller name, a title that describes another item, or an unavailable page. A redirect that still loads is not proof that the listing remains the same.

Save the seller identity and the full item URL rather than a shortened tracking link when possible. Record the date because marketplace listings change. If the page requires a login to reveal options, preserve the information visible in your own lawful session; do not rely on a third-party screenshot whose date and selected SKU are unknown.

Map the SKU before translating it

Taobao variants may combine colour, size and set contents. The lowest card price may belong to an accessory, deposit or smallest option. Select the exact variant on the source page and record the full Chinese option text, a screenshot and any model code. Model codes and measurements often survive translation more accurately than marketing names.

For clothing, capture the size chart and its measurement method. A chart may list flat width, full circumference or a recommended body size; those are not interchangeable. For shoes, note whether the seller uses EU, US, UK or Chinese sizing and whether the option text includes gender or model. For sets, write down every included piece.

Paste the link and compare the imported record

Enter the current Taobao URL in Kakobuy’s product search or purchasing interface. When the product loads, compare the seller, main image, option count, selected text and price with the Taobao page. Imported information can lag or be translated differently. A good match has several consistent fields, not just a familiar cover image.

If a field is missing, do not pick the closest-looking option and hope. Use a concise note that names the source SKU and rejects substitutions. When the page cannot import reliably, use Kakobuy’s current manual or fill-and-buy process if available and follow the fields shown there. The exact interface can change, so the evidence rule matters more than a button label.

Write an order remark that a buyer can execute

A remark should answer a purchasing question. “Please make it good” cannot be verified. “Seller SKU 米白 / 42; no replacement colour; include both laces shown in selected set” is specific. Put measurement requests in the warehouse-inspection stage unless the seller must confirm them before purchase.

If the seller page requires a message about customisation, engraving or a name set, repeat the exact characters and formatting. Customised goods may have limited return options, so confirm that you are prepared to accept the documented choice. Never ask an employee to guess protected brand claims, import legality or personal fit.

Check the first payment as a Taobao order, not a delivered order

Compare the selected Taobao price with the Kakobuy purchase total. Differences can come from the chosen SKU, domestic freight, current exchange presentation or platform charges shown at checkout. Record the live total. Do not assume the international shipment is included; the goods still need to travel from the seller to the warehouse and later from the warehouse to your address.

Follow seller dispatch separately from international tracking

The Taobao seller first sends the item domestically to Kakobuy’s warehouse. Kakobuy’s October 2025 original-order tracking announcement describes using the purchase order number to check seller shipping, dispatch or delivery progress. That purchase-order journey ends at the warehouse; it is not the later international parcel.

If the seller has not dispatched, check stock and the current cancellation process. If domestic tracking shows delivery but the item is not in the warehouse record, provide the purchase order number and last domestic event to support. Specific identifiers are more useful than a screenshot cropped without seller or tracking context.

Use warehouse images to verify the Taobao SKU

Begin by comparing the colour, size tag, model, quantity and set contents with the recorded SKU. Then move to measurements and visible construction. For clothing, request chest width and length when sizing is material. For footwear, compare both shoes, label and sole line. For bags and accessories, check hardware count, dimensions and interior layout.

Kakobuy’s public information says it performs inspection and storage, but images have limits. They cannot prove fibre content, metal grade, authenticity, battery health or long-term durability. Kakobuy also published a QC-image issue notice in May 2026, which is a reminder to confirm that the image belongs to the correct order before judging details.

Compare the warehouse result with the saved source, not today’s page

The Taobao seller may edit the listing after purchase. Use the source screenshot and order record from the transaction date when deciding whether the received variant matches. Today’s page can still help identify a seller update, but it should not overwrite the option you actually ordered.

If the result is wrong or visibly defective, describe the difference in measurable terms and check the current after-sales deadline. Kakobuy’s publicly indexed help describes a five-day return/exchange guarantee for eligible warehouse orders, subject to seller and category conditions. It is not a universal right for every item, especially customised or restricted goods.

Plan the parcel after the Taobao item is accepted

Taobao product pages rarely provide a dependable international packed size. Use seller weight only as an early estimate. Once the item is in the warehouse, compare actual data, packaging and current Kakobuy shipping routes. A lightweight shoe box or puffer jacket can cost more than its scale weight suggests if a route charges dimensional weight.

When consolidating several Taobao orders, check each warehouse deadline and product restriction. Removing retail boxes can reduce volume for some non-fragile goods, but it also removes protection and identifying information. Make that trade-off item by item rather than applying one packing instruction to the whole parcel.

What a reliable Taobao row should contain

  1. A current full source URL and seller identity.
  2. A plain-language product type rather than hype.
  3. The source item or marketplace ID when available.
  4. The date the destination and images were checked.
  5. A note that variants and prices can change.
  6. Category-specific QC questions and measurement fields.
  7. A route to an independent product detail page before checkout.

A huge spreadsheet is not automatically safer than a small one. Ten thousand links with no review date or SKU context can create more ambiguity than a compact catalog with clear records. The best row helps you reproduce the decision: where the item came from, which variant was intended, what arrived and which uncertainty remains.

Final pre-parcel check

Before adding the item to an international parcel, confirm that the saved Taobao option, Kakobuy order and warehouse record describe the same item. Resolve any return question, verify measurements, decide whether original packaging should remain and record the accepted condition. Then compare live parcel routes and terms rather than carrying forward an estimate made before purchase.

This process is slower than blindly pasting a link, but most steps take less than a minute. The time is spent where it has leverage: before payment, before a return window closes and before an international parcel makes correction difficult.
