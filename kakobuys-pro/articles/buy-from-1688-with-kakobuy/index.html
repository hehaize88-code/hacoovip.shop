1688 buying guide

How to Buy from 1688 with Kakobuy: A Practical Guide

A source-first workflow for checking a 1688 offer, importing the right variant into Kakobuy and verifying the warehouse result before shipping.

By Kakobuys.pro Research Desk12 min read1,635 wordsFacts reviewed August 3, 2026

Buying from 1688 with Kakobuy starts with a product link, but the link is only the beginning of the decision. A 1688 offer can contain several specifications, quantities, price levels and seller instructions. If you copy the URL without preserving the selected offer, the purchasing request may be technically valid while still describing the wrong item.

Kakobuy currently names 1688 among the domestic platforms accepted by its search interface. Its public service flow also says users can paste a product link, have goods purchased and delivered to the Kakobuy warehouse, receive a quality check, and later combine accepted warehouse items into an international parcel. This guide turns that broad flow into a record you can verify at every stage. It does not assume a fixed fee, delivery time, return right or shipping route.

The short methodSave the live 1688 offer first. Record the seller, item ID, exact specification, quantity, unit-price condition and date. Then compare those fields with the Kakobuy order and the warehouse record before you approve international shipping.

Understand why a 1688 link needs more context

1688 describes itself in its published terms as a business-to-business information and transaction platform. That context matters because an offer may be written for repeat, wholesale or distribution buyers rather than for one overseas retail purchase. It does not mean every listing requires a large order, and it does not tell you what one seller will accept. It means you should read the quantity and pricing conditions shown on the specific offer instead of assuming that the largest headline number applies to one unit.

Before copying the URL, select the intended colour, size, model, package or material where the page allows it. Record the quantity attached to the displayed price. If the page shows more than one price level, save the level that matches your planned quantity. A screenshot that captures only the lowest number but hides its quantity condition is poor evidence.

Build a source record before importing the link

A useful source record is small enough to make in a minute and detailed enough to resolve a dispute. Save the full 1688 URL, seller or company name, product title, item ID if visible, selected specification text, quantity, displayed unit price, domestic freight information and the date checked. Capture the original Chinese option text alongside any translation. Numbers and model codes may survive translation, but colour and package descriptions can shift.

Do not treat seller badges or a polished product page as proof of material quality, authenticity or export suitability. Those questions require separate evidence. The source record answers a narrower question: what offer did you intend the purchasing agent to act on?

Paste the 1688 link into Kakobuy and compare fields

Use Kakobuy’s current product-link search or purchasing interface and paste the complete 1688 URL. When the product record loads, compare the seller, main image, option labels, selected quantity and price condition with the source page. A matching cover photo is not enough; suppliers can use similar images, and one listing can contain many materially different specifications.

If the importer leaves out an option or the offer does not load correctly, do not choose the nearest-looking choice. Kakobuy’s public navigation currently includes a “Fill & Buy” route; use the fields displayed there rather than guessing when a normal import is insufficient. Interface labels can change, so follow the current screen and write a purchasing remark that can be executed without interpretation.

Write an order remark that eliminates substitutions

An effective remark uses observable terms. State the selected Chinese specification, model code, quantity and whether substitutions are allowed. If a colour must match a source image, identify the image or option text rather than saying “the nice one.” If the listing offers sample, custom and stock versions, name the one you intend. For a mixed order, list the quantity of each option separately.

Avoid asking staff to determine authenticity, legal import status or personal fit. Those are not simple purchasing instructions. For restricted items, batteries, liquids or branded goods, check the current platform and logistics restrictions before payment. Route eligibility can depend on the item, destination and parcel, so an old community answer is not a reliable approval.

Separate the product order from the international parcel

The first transaction covers the product-order stage shown by Kakobuy. The supplier then sends the item domestically to the Kakobuy warehouse. International freight is a later decision after the warehouse record exists and you select items for a parcel. Keeping these stages separate prevents a low unit price from being mistaken for a delivered cost.

Build an early budget with distinct lines: item subtotal, supplier-to-warehouse delivery if shown, any current charges displayed by Kakobuy, an international-freight scenario, and possible destination charges. Use the live Kakobuy shipping calculator for planning, but treat the result as an estimate based on the inputs you provide. The warehouse weight, packed dimensions, item classification and eligible routes can change the later quote.

Use the warehouse record to test the 1688 order

When the goods arrive, start with identity rather than finish. Compare the quantity, visible specification, colour, size label, model code and package contents with the saved source record. If the order contains several identical units, confirm the count and look for variation between them. For mixed specifications, match each unit to the order breakdown.

Then move to category-specific inspection. Clothing needs measurements, panel shape, seams and print placement. Shoes need pair symmetry, labels, sole joins and size evidence. Electronics need the exact model and plug or interface information, but photos cannot prove battery condition or full function. A warehouse image is evidence of what is visible in that frame, not a certificate of materials, authenticity or long-term performance.

Handle quantity, defects and seller questions early

If the quantity or specification is wrong, describe the difference using the order record: “ordered three units of specification X; warehouse record shows two,” or “ordered model A; label shows model B.” Measurable language is more useful than “not as expected.” Attach the source screenshot, Kakobuy purchase-order number and relevant warehouse image when contacting support.

Do not assume that a 1688 seller will accept every return, partial return or customised-item request. Check the current Kakobuy after-sales screen and the conditions attached to the actual order. If a deadline is displayed, act within it. This article deliberately avoids repeating a universal return period because seller, category, customisation and current platform rules can affect eligibility.

Decide whether consolidation helps this parcel

Kakobuy’s public flow says users can select warehouse products and combine them into one parcel. Consolidation can simplify shipment, but it is not automatically cheaper. More units can increase actual weight, while boxes, rigid packaging and irregular shapes can increase dimensional weight. One restricted item may also change the available routes for everything grouped with it.

Compare at least two parcel plans when the order is bulky: keep supplier packaging where it protects the goods, or remove non-essential packaging where the platform permits and damage risk remains acceptable. Use current warehouse dimensions and live route terms. Do not use a universal volumetric divisor; the relevant charging rule is the one displayed for the eligible route you are considering.

A practical 1688-to-Kakobuy checklist

  1. Open the live 1688 offer and confirm it still describes the intended product.
  2. Save the full URL, seller, item ID, date and source screenshot.
  3. Record the exact specification, quantity and price condition.
  4. Paste the link into Kakobuy and compare more than the main image.
  5. Use a precise remark when any imported field is incomplete.
  6. Keep the product-order budget separate from international freight.
  7. At warehouse intake, verify identity and quantity before quality details.
  8. Resolve a mismatch before the applicable after-sales opportunity closes.
  9. Compare packed measurements, restrictions and live routes before shipping.
  10. Keep the source, order, warehouse and parcel records together.

Final decision

The safest way to buy from 1688 with Kakobuy is not to trust an importer blindly or to avoid it entirely. It is to create an evidence chain. The 1688 page defines the intended offer; the Kakobuy purchase order records the request; warehouse evidence shows what arrived; and the parcel screen defines the shipping decision available at that time.

That chain does not guarantee seller performance or eliminate international-shipping risk. It does make errors easier to spot while correction is still possible. Most of the work happens before payment: preserving the right specification, quantity and price condition instead of asking a later photo to reconstruct an unclear order.
