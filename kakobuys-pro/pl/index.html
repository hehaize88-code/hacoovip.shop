Is Kakobuys.pro the official Kakobuy website?+

No. Kakobuys.pro is an independent research and product-discovery directory. It is not operated by or endorsed by Kakobuy, Taobao, Weidian, 1688 or the brands mentioned in third-party listings.

Where do the shopping buttons go?+

Product and category buttons open the corresponding destination listing or category. Kakobuys.pro does not process orders or payments.

Does a QC photo guarantee product quality?+

No. A QC photo can help you spot visible issues such as asymmetry, poor print placement or incorrect measurements, but it cannot reliably prove material composition, long-term durability or authenticity.

How long does Kakobuy store items?+

Kakobuy’s publicly indexed information pages currently mention 100 days of free warehouse storage. Policies can change, so confirm the current deadline inside the service before placing or consolidating an order.
