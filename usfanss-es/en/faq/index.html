What is a USFans spreadsheet?+

An organised collection of product links. This site turns it into a faster, mobile-friendly visual experience.

Does this site sell products?+

No. It is an independent discovery guide linking every selection to its matching listing.

Are prices and stock final?+

No. They can change. Always check the final listing, its variants and availability before deciding.

What should I check in QC photos?+

Colour, size, measurements, stitching, finish, labels and every detail relevant to your purchase.

How many QC photos does USFans include?+

Many public listings state 3 to 7 free HD QC photos. Always confirm the terms shown on the specific listing when ordering.

Can I request a return after warehouse arrival?+

Some listings mention a five-day request window. It is not guaranteed acceptance; seller terms, item type, condition and costs still apply.

How is shipping to Spain calculated?+

It depends on billable weight, volume, packaging, available line and destination. Mainland Spain, Balearic Islands, Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla may differ.

Does Spanish VAT apply?+

VAT may apply to imports from outside the EU. Check whether IOSS includes it or whether the carrier collects it with possible handling charges.

What changes on 1 July 2026?+

Spain's Tax Agency states that purchases up to €150 shipped from outside the EU face a fixed €3 duty per item category, in addition to applicable VAT.

Should every item be consolidated?+

Not always. One bulky or restricted item can raise the price or limit routes, so compare combined and separate parcels first.

Is the estimate the final price?+

No. Packed weight and dimensions, optional services, route and destination charges can change the total.

When were the displayed products checked?+

The six links, names, first images, categories and USD prices were verified against the destination catalogue on 13 August 2026.
