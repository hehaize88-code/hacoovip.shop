Which QC photos are you looking for?

Photos of my Sugargoo order. Open the received item in your account’s Packing Center. Match the order line, colour and size before opening its inspection photos. If the seller’s tracking shows delivery but your item has no pictures, check receipt and photo status. Sugargoo shows the warehouse stage in its official ordering guide.

Photos for a listing before I buy. Start with its marketplace and original item ID. Compare a public set only when it matches the listing, then check whether the pictured size or colour is identified. Our shoulder-bag example with three warehouse photos shows which details you can check and which still need a measurement. A catalogue thumbnail by itself supplies no inspection evidence.

No matching set? Record “no verified QC for this variant.” Save the original listing and its size diagram. Once your order reaches the warehouse, compare the actual item and ask for the one missing angle that would change your decision.
