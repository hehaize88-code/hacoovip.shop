Check one product link before you ship

Open the seller listing behind your W2C (“where to cop”) link. Match its marketplace item ID, shop and selected option against your order, then look at the matching warehouse item. A catalogue’s own reference number helps you find its record again. It cannot identify a seller’s size or colour on its own.

  • Shoes. The shoe record 6045 has a catalogue image. Follow the source link to identify the shoe and selected size. For a fit decision, ask for a heel-to-toe insole measurement on the received pair.
  • Clothing. For the T-shirt record 5931, compare the exact colour and size with your order. If the seller gives a flat chest width, measure underarm to underarm on the warehouse photo using the same method.
  • Bags. Match the bag size and strap option first. Use the bag QC checklist and photo example to decide whether you need another angle before shipping.
