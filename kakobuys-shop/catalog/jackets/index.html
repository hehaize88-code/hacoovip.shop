Kakobuy Jackets Spreadsheet.

Use this page to narrow your search and review the most useful visible checks for seasonal shells, puffers and overshirts.

What to verify before you rely on a listing

A spreadsheet entry is a starting point, not proof that the seller, variant or price is unchanged. Open the destination and compare the item ID, selected colour, size range and current seller information. Treat a missing or unrelated destination as expired.

For jackets the most useful warehouse photos usually include a full front or rear view, construction details, the size label and a measurement image. One flattering angle is rarely enough.

Three visible checks that matter

1. Zip trackCompare the photo with the ordered variant. Look for inconsistent placement, visible damage or dimensions that do not match the seller’s chart. Ask for clarification when the relevant area is not shown.
2. Panel alignmentCompare the photo with the ordered variant. Look for inconsistent placement, visible damage or dimensions that do not match the seller’s chart. Ask for clarification when the relevant area is not shown.
3. Fill balanceCompare the photo with the ordered variant. Look for inconsistent placement, visible damage or dimensions that do not match the seller’s chart. Ask for clarification when the relevant area is not shown.

What QC photos cannot prove

Photos cannot reliably prove fibre content, long-term wear, waterproofing, electrical safety or authenticity. Use QC as a visible-defect filter, not as a laboratory test or guarantee.

Use the destination responsibly

The category button opens a separate destination. Check current terms, restricted-item rules, shipping estimates and after-sales windows before deciding.
