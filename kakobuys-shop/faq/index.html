Frequently asked questions

Short answers, no invented certainty.

The policies cited here were reviewed on July 29, 2026. Always confirm time-sensitive terms in the relevant service before acting.

Scope & sources

Is Kakobuys.shop the official Kakobuy website?+

No. Kakobuys.shop is an independent research and product-discovery directory. It is not operated by or endorsed by Kakobuy, Taobao, Weidian, 1688 or the brands mentioned in third-party listings.

Where do the shopping buttons go?+

Product and category buttons open the corresponding destination listing or category. Kakobuys.shop does not process orders or payments.

Does a QC photo guarantee product quality?+

No. A QC photo can help you spot visible issues such as asymmetry, poor print placement or incorrect measurements, but it cannot reliably prove material composition, long-term durability or authenticity.

How long does Kakobuy store items?+

Kakobuy’s publicly indexed information pages currently mention 100 days of free warehouse storage. Policies can change, so confirm the current deadline inside the service before placing or consolidating an order.

Can every warehouse item be returned within five days?+

No. Kakobuy describes a five-day return/exchange guarantee for eligible third-party seller items. Seller participation, item condition and marketplace restrictions can affect eligibility.

What costs are separate when ordering through Kakobuy?+

Kakobuy’s publicly indexed service-fee summary separates item cost—product price plus domestic shipping to the warehouse—from international freight. International freight is calculated later using the parcel, route and destination.

Does Kakobuy provide package photos before dispatch?+

A Kakobuy help entry says its package-photo service provides three photographs of the packed parcel before shipment. Package photos document the parcel; they should not be confused with item-level QC images.

Does Kakobuy QC test electronics?+

Kakobuy’s publicly indexed inspection information says inspection of household appliances, digital products and other electronics does not include verifying authenticity, function or quality. Confirm specifications and restrictions separately.

What should I do if a QC image is wrong or unavailable?+

Stop the product review and compare the image with the order record. Kakobuy’s May 10, 2026 notice acknowledged abnormal displays, incorrect images and temporarily unavailable QC images. Ask for the correct evidence before dispatch.

Is there one fixed Kakobuy shipping price per kilogram?+

No reliable universal price applies to every parcel. Kakobuy says international freight depends on weight, shipping method and destination; parcel dimensions, restrictions and optional services can also change the routes and final amount shown.
