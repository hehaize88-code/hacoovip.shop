How to Buy from 1688 with Kakobuy: MOQ, Batch and Variant Checks

A practical 1688-to-Kakobuy guide for checking product links, minimum and batch quantities, price tiers, variants, buyer notes and warehouse evidence.

By Kakobuys.shop Research Desk9 min read1,714 wordsFact-checked August 3, 2026

Buying from 1688 is not simply the Taobao workflow with a different domain. Alibaba Group describes 1688 as a domestic wholesale marketplace connecting buyers and suppliers across many categories. That wholesale setting changes what must be checked: the displayed amount may belong to a quantity tier, the seller may require a minimum order, and a “quantity of one” in the interface may represent one piece, one pack or one batch.

Kakobuy publicly lists 1688 among the marketplaces it can purchase from. Its public web application also contains special cart logic for 1688 listings that uses a seller-provided batch_number as the minimum and quantity step when that field exists. This is useful evidence that batch multiples are handled by the interface, but it is not proof that every listing is available, correctly translated or suitable for a single-item order. The safe workflow is to reconcile the source listing, Kakobuy import and warehouse record as three separate stages.

Wholesale price is not a complete order instructionRecord the exact source URL, seller, unit, selected variant, quantity tier and batch multiple. If any one of those fields is unclear, pause before payment.

Start with a final 1688 product URL

Open the shared link and let any short link or tracking redirect finish. Save the final item URL and the numeric product ID if it is visible. Also record the seller or company name and the date you checked the listing. A spreadsheet image or social-media preview can help discovery, but it cannot preserve a changed option menu or quantity rule.

If the destination becomes a search page, login wall or unrelated product, do not guess a replacement. Kakobuy has a public “item not found” flow, yet its public disclaimer says it cannot guarantee product quality, authenticity, completeness or seller fulfilment. Treat assistance with a broken link as a way to locate evidence, not as verification that a substitute is equivalent.

Separate MOQ, batch multiple and price tier

These three ideas are often collapsed into “minimum order,” but they answer different questions. MOQ is the minimum total quantity the seller will accept. A batch multiple is the increment in which the quantity must increase. A price tier is the unit price associated with a quantity range. One listing can use all three.

For example, suppose a listing shows a minimum of 6 pieces, a batch multiple of 3 and different unit prices at 6, 30 and 100 pieces. Valid quantities begin at 6 and then follow the permitted step: 6, 9, 12 and so on. A quantity of 7 may be rejected or automatically adjusted. The 100-piece unit price must not be used to budget an order of 6. These numbers are an editorial calculation example, not a quotation from a live seller.

Quantity checkIf a listing exposes a batch multiple, calculate valid quantity = minimum + (whole number × step). Then confirm that Kakobuy displays the same final quantity and unit before payment.

Identify what the seller means by one unit

The word “piece” in a translation may hide the commercial unit. Look for packaging language in the original listing: single item, pair, set, pack, box, metre, kilogram or carton. A product photo showing twelve items does not prove that twelve are included. Likewise, a low option price may refer to a sample, accessory, replacement part or deposit rather than the main product.

Write the unit into your record in plain language: “3 packs, 10 clips per pack,” or “6 individual shirts, mixed sizes allowed only if seller confirms.” If mixed colours or sizes count toward an MOQ, preserve the seller’s rule rather than assuming Kakobuy can combine options automatically.

Map every 1688 variant before importing

Record the original option text, model code, colour, size and any material or grade. Keep Chinese characters when they distinguish variants; add a translated explanation beside them. For clothing, save the size chart and measurement unit. For components, record dimensions, connector, voltage or model compatibility. For a set, list the included parts.

Do not choose by thumbnail alone. Sellers may reuse a hero image across materials or pack sizes, and machine translation can turn distinct colour or specification terms into the same English phrase. A source screenshot with the selected option highlighted is stronger than a nickname copied from a spreadsheet.

Compare the Kakobuy import line by line

Paste the final 1688 URL into Kakobuy and compare the imported seller context, source image, option names, unit price for the intended tier, domestic delivery if shown, minimum quantity and quantity step. Kakobuy’s current public app code adjusts 1688 quantities around a batch_number when one is supplied. That adjustment should be visible in the order you are about to pay for; do not rely on invisible correction later.

Recalculate the merchandise subtotal from the final quantity and selected tier. Keep the later international parcel separate. The source price, domestic transport, optional services, international freight and possible destination charges are different rows, and none should be inferred from the cheapest number on the listing.

Use a buyer note that can be verified

A useful note tells the purchasing employee exactly what to select and what not to substitute. Include the original option text, model or colour code, required quantity, commercial unit and a stop condition. For example: “款式 B, black, 6 individual pieces; batch step 3; do not replace with 6 packs or another material.” Avoid requests such as “best quality,” which cannot resolve an ambiguous listing.

If stock needs confirmation, name the exact variant and quantity. Kakobuy’s indexed help says inventory may be double-checked with the seller and remains subject to the seller’s actual stock. A seller reply can support availability at that moment; it cannot guarantee the later warehouse item’s measurements or construction.

Audit the warehouse record as a quantity order

Kakobuy says that items arriving at its warehouse receive quality inspection and storage, with status available to the user. Treat this as a platform description of its process, not independent proof that every issue will be detected. Start by matching the purchase-order number, seller, item and selected options. Then count the commercial units and the pieces inside each unit if the listing promised a pack.

After identity and quantity, move to category-specific QC. Check labels and measurements for size-sensitive goods; dimensions, connectors and markings for components; and included parts for sets. Standard photos may not show a pack count, hidden component or measurement. Ask for the single extra view or measurement that could change the accept-or-return decision, while checking the live order state and any applicable deadline.

Plan consolidation only after the units are known

Wholesale packs can change parcel planning. Ten small retail items may arrive as one dense carton; lightweight display materials may create more volume than weight suggests. Wait for warehouse weight and dimensions before choosing an international line. Compare protected and compact packing only when removing packaging will not make the pieces difficult to count, identify or protect.

Do not assume that a route accepting one sample will accept a larger commercial-looking quantity. Read the current line restrictions and destination rules for the actual category and quantity. Kakobuy’s availability of a purchase link does not decide customs treatment, tax, intellectual-property risk or import eligibility in the destination country.

Know when to stop the order

Pause when the final URL no longer identifies the recorded product, the unit cannot be translated confidently, the cart quantity conflicts with the seller’s MOQ, the chosen tier is not reflected in the subtotal, or the imported options do not preserve the source code. Also stop if the order only works by assuming that unrelated variants can be mixed.

A missing answer is not a reason to select the closest option. It is a reason to request clarification, choose a clearer listing or remove the row from a spreadsheet. A transparent “unavailable as checked on August 3, 2026” is more useful than a live-looking link that no longer supports the recorded decision.

1688-to-Kakobuy pre-payment checklist

  1. The final 1688 URL and item ID match the product recorded in the spreadsheet.
  2. The seller and fact-check date are saved.
  3. The commercial unit is explicit: piece, pair, set, pack, metre, kilogram or carton.
  4. MOQ, batch multiple and price tier are recorded as separate fields.
  5. The selected original-language variant, model, colour and size are preserved.
  6. Kakobuy shows the intended final quantity, option and merchandise subtotal.
  7. The buyer note includes a stop condition and forbids an unapproved substitute.
  8. Warehouse QC will check identity, quantity, pack contents and category-specific details.
  9. International shipping is budgeted only after realistic weight, dimensions and route rules are available.

The central discipline is reconciliation. A 1688 listing is useful when its wholesale fields survive the move into the purchasing order and can still be checked at the warehouse. If the link, unit, quantity, tier or variant changes meaning between stages, correct that field before spending more.
