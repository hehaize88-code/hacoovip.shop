Weidian link guide

How to Buy from Weidian with Kakobuy: Link, Variant and Seller Checks

A careful Weidian-to-Kakobuy workflow for preserving item IDs, seller context, variant instructions, warehouse evidence and parcel decisions.

By Kakobuys.shop Research Desk12 min read1,346 wordsReviewed July 30, 2026

Weidian listings often reach buyers through a spreadsheet, image post or seller album before the original product page is opened. That makes the first verification unusually important. The English nickname may describe a style, while the destination contains several batches, colour codes or price options that are not obvious outside the marketplace page.

Kakobuy publicly identifies Weidian among its agent-purchasing contexts and says it purchases from a URL supplied by the user. The agent can execute the submitted listing and instructions, but it does not remove the need to verify the seller, item ID and selected variant. This guide shows how to keep those fields connected from the link to the warehouse.

Do not order from the preview aloneOpen the original Weidian destination, record the shop, item ID, selected option text and current price, then compare the imported Kakobuy page. A familiar image is not enough.

Extract the stable parts of a Weidian link

A shared URL can include referral or campaign parameters. Preserve the actual item destination and record the item ID shown in the page or URL. Also save the seller or shop name and order date. These identifiers help distinguish a temporary image-host problem from a genuinely changed listing.

If a short link expands to a different domain or item, stop. Do not “repair” an unknown link by guessing a similar product. Search the exact item ID or ask the person who shared it for the original destination. A spreadsheet row without a recoverable source should be marked unavailable rather than quietly replaced.

Read the listing for batch and option language

Some Weidian sellers use model or batch codes that matter more than the translated product name. Record those codes exactly. If the listing separates deposit, replacement part, special size or full product, make sure the selected price belongs to the intended item. The lowest visible amount can be attached to an option that is not a complete product.

For colour, take a screenshot of the selected thumbnail and option text. For footwear, record the sizing system and any instruction about half sizes. For clothing, preserve the size chart and measurement units. For an accessory set, list every promised component instead of writing “full set,” which may have different meanings across sellers.

Compare source and import field by field

Paste the Weidian URL into Kakobuy and let the purchasing interface load the product. Compare five fields: source image, seller context, option list, price for the selected option and domestic shipping if shown. If four fields match but the option text is truncated, use a precise remark. If the product imports as an unrelated item, do not pay.

Machine translation can alter proper nouns and colour names. A numeric item ID, seller code and selected source text are better anchors. Keep the original characters in your remark when they identify the option. Add an English explanation after them rather than replacing them.

Use remarks for decisions, not hopes

Good instructions tell the purchasing employee what to select or reject: “款式 B, black, EU 43; do not substitute another batch.” Bad instructions ask for an outcome the buyer cannot verify: “Make sure it is perfect.” Separate purchasing instructions from later warehouse QC requests so neither becomes buried in a paragraph.

If you want the seller to confirm stock, include the exact option. If you want the warehouse to measure an insole or garment, request that after receipt through the current inspection process. A seller’s answer and a warehouse measurement are different types of evidence.

Understand the domestic waiting period

After the product payment, the purchasing order still depends on seller confirmation and domestic delivery. Kakobuy’s indexed purchasing help mentions a normal working-day range for the URL-based service, but individual stock, seller dispatch and domestic carriers vary. Do not convert that range into an international delivery promise.

Use the purchase order number when asking about seller progress. Kakobuy’s original-order tracking announcement says this number can be used to check shipping, dispatch or delivery updates with the seller. Once the item reaches the warehouse, a different record governs inspection and later parcel creation.

Check the warehouse record against the Weidian evidence

First confirm that the photo belongs to your purchase order. Then check model, colour, size, quantity and included parts. A batch code visible on a box or tag can support identification, but a label alone does not prove the product’s authenticity or internal construction.

Move from identity to visible QC. For shoes, compare both toe shapes, heels, sole bond and size labels, and request a measurement when the sizing system is unclear. For clothing, compare shoulder and side seams, print placement, chest width and length. For bags, inspect body shape, handles, hardware and interior layout. Ask for the one angle that can change your decision.

Recognise what a Weidian seller history cannot prove

Sales counts, follower numbers and community recommendations can provide context, but they do not guarantee the next order. Listings, staff, material and batch can change. Review dates matter. A community QC photo from another buyer is most useful when it identifies the same seller, item, option and recent purchase period.

Do not call a seller “verified” unless you define the test. It is more honest to say that a destination was reachable on a date, the item ID matched, and specific public QC images were reviewed. That wording leaves room for change and tells the reader what was actually checked.

Handle a mismatch before the deadline

If the warehouse item differs from the recorded option, show the source screenshot, Kakobuy order and warehouse evidence together. State the mismatch without exaggeration. A clear case is “source and order say EU 43; warehouse label says EU 42.” A weak case is “these look wrong” with no selected option.

Kakobuy’s public help describes a five-day return/exchange guarantee for eligible warehouse orders, but seller agreement and category restrictions matter. Check the live order state and deadline. Customised items and some seller policies may not support the same remedy.

Budget the international parcel separately

A Weidian price is not a delivered price. Keep the product, domestic delivery, international freight, optional services and possible destination charges in separate rows. Use Kakobuy’s current shipping calculator for a pre-purchase range, then use warehouse or packed measurements for the parcel decision.

Rigid shoe boxes, bags that should not be crushed and light padded jackets can create dimensional weight. Removing a box may reduce volume but also protection. Compare compact and protected scenarios when possible, then read the live route’s weight rules, restrictions and compensation terms.

A useful Weidian spreadsheet checklist

  1. The destination opens the same item ID recorded in the row.
  2. The seller or shop is named and the review date is visible.
  3. The row distinguishes product, batch and selected option.
  4. Price is labelled as variable and tied to a specific option.
  5. QC guidance is specific to the category.
  6. Community photos are dated and not called proof of future quality.
  7. Dead or redirected links are removed rather than silently repointed.

The safest way to use Weidian with Kakobuy is not to memorise a sequence of buttons. Interfaces change. Keep the source ID, option evidence, purchasing instruction, warehouse record and parcel choice connected. If one link in that chain stops matching, pause there instead of hoping the next stage will correct it.
