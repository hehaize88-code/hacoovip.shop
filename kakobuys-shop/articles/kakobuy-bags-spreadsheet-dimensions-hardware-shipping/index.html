Kakobuy Bags Spreadsheet: Dimensions, Hardware and Shipping

Turn bag links into comparable records by separating usable size, structure, closures, straps, QC evidence and packing risk.

By Kakobuys.shop Research Desk13 min read1,439 wordsFact-checked August 27, 2026

A Kakobuy bags spreadsheet should help a beginner answer three questions before ordering: will the bag hold what it needs to hold, are the important components identifiable in the listing, and what should be checked before international shipping? A thumbnail and a headline price answer none of those questions reliably.

Kakobuy’s current public service information describes purchasing goods from Chinese domestic websites, receiving them at a warehouse, performing quality inspection and providing photographs before later international shipment. That workflow supports a structured bag record, but it does not make every listing detail true or every hidden feature inspectable. The spreadsheet’s job is to preserve evidence and show gaps.

Save ruleA bag earns a verified row only when the exact source item, selected option, dimensions, closure, strap or handle configuration and current price are attached to a check date. Unknown fields remain unknown.

Begin with the use case, not the silhouette

Write one intended use before comparing links: daily essentials, laptop commute, short travel, evening carry or equipment storage. Then list the objects that must fit. A “medium” bag can fail because its opening is too narrow, the base is shallow or the interior is divided, even when the outside width looks sufficient.

Use a simple object test. Record the largest required object’s width, height and depth, then compare it with the bag’s interior evidence. Exterior dimensions are not usable capacity. Lining, padding, seams, curved sides and compartments consume space. If only external measurements are available, label capacity as unverified.

Keep the original unit and convert in a separate column. Define endpoints instead of copying “length” or “size.” If a listing shows multiple sizes, connect every dimension set to the exact selected option. Do not average values or borrow measurements from a similar-looking seller page.

Separate body structure from packing shape

Classify the bag as soft, semi-structured or rigid based on visible construction and listing information. Then record how it is shown: empty, stuffed, folded or supported. A soft tote can look deep when filled with paper but collapse when empty. A structured bag may keep its shape yet consume more parcel volume.

Do not promise that creases will disappear or that a rigid panel cannot bend. Use packing condition as a risk field: low when the form can be compressed without affecting the intended result, medium when shape depends on careful filling, and high when corners, frames or molded panels need support. This is an editorial planning label, not a Kakobuy guarantee.

Map every closure and access point

Record the main closure type—zip, flap, magnet, drawstring, clasp, buckle or open top—and any secondary compartment closures. A listing photo may show a decorative buckle while the actual access uses a magnetic snap. Save both function and appearance separately.

For zips, note direction, number of pullers and whether the opening reaches the bag corners. For flaps, note the visible fastening point. For drawstrings, note whether the channel and ends appear complete. A closure photo can show alignment and visible parts; it cannot prove long-term smoothness, magnet strength or weather resistance.

Build a hardware inventory before QC

List the hardware that must be present: handle rings, strap clips, buckles, feet, zip pulls, rivets, decorative plates and detachable pieces. Connect each item to a listing image or option description. “Gold hardware” is incomplete when the exact option offers several finishes.

Warehouse photos can help confirm quantity, visible finish, gross alignment and obvious scratches when lighting is adequate. Reflections can look like damage, so compare more than one angle. A photograph cannot verify metal composition, plating thickness, load strength or long-term corrosion resistance. Keep those claims out of the verified column.

Check handles and straps as load paths

A detachable strap needs attachment points, clips and adjustment hardware. A fixed handle needs visible anchors. Record handle count, strap count, adjustability, minimum and maximum length if measured, attachment type and whether spare pieces are shown. Do not assume a photographed accessory is included unless the option or package list supports it.

For QC, inspect both paired attachments, surrounding seams, edge finish and symmetry. A wide view shows whether a handle is twisted; a closer view can show an open stitch or missing rivet. Neither predicts how the assembly will perform under a heavy load. The spreadsheet can flag a decision-changing gap without claiming a strength test occurred.

Preserve interior and accessory evidence

Record lining color, compartment count, visible pockets, organizers, dust bag, keys, locks, charms or removable pouches only when the selected listing supports them. Use “shown,” “listed,” “not shown” and “unclear” rather than a yes/no field that turns missing evidence into absence.

Interior photographs often need a specific request because a closed or stuffed bag hides the base and seams. Ask for one view that shows the complete interior, pocket layout and any removable component together. Avoid requesting private labels or authenticity claims that warehouse photos cannot establish.

Plan the QC sequence before the bag arrives

  1. Match source item, color, size and accessory option.
  2. Review the whole silhouette from front, back, sides and base.
  3. Confirm key dimensions with named endpoints when they affect use.
  4. Count handles, straps, clips and removable parts.
  5. Inspect closures, corner shape, panel seams and attachments.
  6. Check interior layout and included accessories.
  7. Record what the photos cannot prove.

Kakobuy’s public inspection wording supports visible warehouse checks, but this guide does not state a universal photo count, extra-photo fee or response time. Use the current order screen for available services. Ask for an extra image only when it can change the keep, clarify or after-sales decision.

Add parcel-planning fields without inventing freight

Product price is not delivered cost. Add seller-to-warehouse delivery, listed or measured product weight, packed dimensions when available, protection requirement and packaging-removal decision as separate fields. International freight belongs to the later parcel stage and depends on live destination, route, packed weight, volume and rules.

For a soft bag, compare a protected natural-shape scenario with a modestly compressed scenario only if the material and structure allow it. For a rigid bag, compare internal support and corner protection against the extra parcel volume. Never publish one universal dimensional divisor or promise that removing packaging is safe. Test the actual parcel in the current estimator after warehouse measurement.

Compare listings without calling them identical

Two links that reuse the same image can still differ in seller, batch, option, size, material claim, included parts, domestic delivery and after-sales terms. Create columns for evidence quality rather than declaring duplicates. A cheaper record with missing dimensions and unclear accessories may be less useful than a slightly higher-priced record with a complete option and measurable specification.

Use three statuses. “Ready” means the exact option, dimensions, components and current amounts are clear. “Clarify” means one answer or targeted image can resolve a gap. “Reject” means identity, size, option or included parts cannot be matched. Apply the same fields to every candidate.

Kakobuy bags spreadsheet checklist

  1. Name the intended use and required objects.
  2. Save the exact source URL, seller, option and check date.
  3. Define width, height, depth, opening and strap-drop endpoints.
  4. Keep exterior size separate from usable interior capacity.
  5. Record structure and current packing shape.
  6. Map closures, handles, straps and every removable component.
  7. Inventory hardware without making material claims.
  8. Write the QC sequence before warehouse arrival.
  9. Separate product, domestic-delivery and later parcel costs.
  10. Use ready, clarify or reject based on evidence completeness.

A strong Kakobuy bags spreadsheet is not a gallery of fashionable silhouettes. It is a comparison tool where dimensions have endpoints, hardware has an inventory, accessories have evidence and packing risk is visible before the parcel is built. That helps a beginner discover products without confusing a promising image with a verified order.
