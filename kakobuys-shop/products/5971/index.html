QC questions for this item

Use the photo sequence to answer concrete questions for this product type. If the needed angle or measurement is missing, request it instead of guessing.

Crown and brim alignment

View the hat straight from the front and side. Crown panels should meet evenly, and the brim should sit centrally without a visible twist or one raised edge.

Embroidery, print and seams

Use the centre front and panel joins to judge artwork placement. Inspect edge stitching, thread breaks and pattern continuity across seams.

Circumference and closure

Confirm the inner label and ordered size. For fitted hats request circumference; for adjustable caps inspect the buckle, snap or hook-and-loop closure.
