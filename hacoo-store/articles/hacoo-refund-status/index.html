Hacoo Refund Status: From Your Request to the Payment

Find the right Hacoo order, document a wrong or damaged item, follow return instructions and tell a refund approval apart from money reaching your account.

Hacoo Store Guide · Updated November 2, 2026

Find the order and the last action recorded

Open the original order before contacting anyone about a Hacoo refund. Find the item, selected option and amount actually charged. Then open any after-sales conversation attached to that order. Write down the most recent action and its date. “I sent photos on Monday and received a request for the packaging label” tells you what to do next. “My refund has been pending since Monday” could describe several quite different stages and can lead both you and a support agent to investigate the wrong one.

Check who took the payment. A product guide, creator post or spreadsheet may have sent you to another merchant. The name on the order confirmation is a better starting point than the post where you discovered the item. If you bought through a separate service, open that service's order record and return instructions. If the purchase was made in Hacoo, enter the app through your normal route. Keep the order reference nearby and use the support options associated with the purchase. Public comments are a poor place to share a receipt or a shipping label.

Describe the issue in terms of one item

Separate what you ordered from what arrived. For a wrong colour, record the colour name on your order and the label on the product bag. For the wrong size, photograph the garment's or shoe's own label as well. A picture showing the item next to its associated packaging helps when several orders arrived together. Capture a whole-item view before taking a close-up of the problem. Someone unfamiliar with the product should be able to tell which part of the item the close-up shows.

The official Help Center asks for different evidence for damage, an incorrect product and a missing item. Use that distinction to keep your account useful. For damage, keep the outer and inner packaging until you receive instructions and photograph the affected area in good light. For a missing accessory, show what came in the package and identify the part shown in the order description. If an order contains several parcels, check the parcel breakdown first. One box without a second item does not establish that the second item was meant to be in that box.

Keep originals of the photographs and send the copies requested in the order conversation. Leave labels readable where support needs them. Crop unrelated household details and avoid posting the photographs publicly. A short note listing the item, the problem seen on arrival and the requested outcome is easier to follow than a long account of every attempt to get help. If a problem only appears during use, describe when it occurs and what you observed. Avoid giving a technical diagnosis when a clear description will do.

Read the instructions for this return

Hacoo's public policy pages describe more than one route. Its shipping page discusses after-sales help and situations where the customer keeps the item. Its dedicated returns policy also describes cases in which the company asks for the goods back. That same policy distinguishes a change of mind from a damaged or incorrect item, with packaging, condition and cost provisions depending on the case. Use those pages to understand which questions to ask, then read the instructions issued on your particular order.

Record any deadline in the order view when you begin. The public returns policy refers to a 15-day period after receipt for certain requests, subject to the item and conditions it describes. If you are still gathering photographs, contact the official order channel promptly instead of assuming an open message extends that window. Keep the item, packaging, accessories and tags together until you know which pieces the instructions cover. For a multiple-item order, label the correct bag now. Two similar barcodes are easy to mix up later.

Wait for the case-specific address or label before sending anything. The sender shown on the original parcel may be different from the location authorised to receive a return. If the instructions are unclear about postage, ask who arranges it and whether any expense is covered before paying a carrier. Where you are told to keep the item, save that message with the case. These details matter more to the next step than a general claim elsewhere that returns are always free or always require shipping.

Follow the request one decision at a time

A submission records that you asked for help. A message asking for another photograph is a request for evidence. An approval may authorise a return shipment or confirm an amount. Look at the verb and the object of the message together. “Return approved” can mean you may now send the item. “Refund issued to the original payment method” describes a later action. App wording can change, so follow what your screen says rather than trying to match it to a fixed list of status colours.

Imagine that two shirts arrived, one correct and one in the wrong size. Your message names only the second shirt. Support replies with a return label for that line item. Match the label to the shirt recorded in the case and check whether any accessories must accompany it. Keep a picture of the contents and the carrier receipt if the instructions require you to ship. A carrier delivery scan shows movement of a parcel. Reopen the case to see whether the contents have been checked and the refund itself has been issued.

Match the approved amount to the transaction

Read the amount and currency in the decision. Compare them with the amount allocated to the affected item, taking account of any discount or other adjustment shown on the original receipt. A price on today's product page cannot tell you exactly what you paid for that item in a previous basket. If only part of a set is missing, look for the amount actually approved. When it differs from what you expected, ask for the calculation and name the relevant line on the receipt.

Check the named destination of the money. A message about an account balance, a credit card refund and a payment-provider refund each points you to a different place. Hacoo's public guidance includes both a balance workflow and separate payment-method timings. Follow the destination on your own decision. An available promotional credit or review reward can appear in an account without being the refund in question. Match the amount, transaction description and date before treating any balance change as confirmation that the case is complete.

Read the date that starts the waiting period

For a request still under review, record the date it was submitted and the date of your last reply. For a required return, record the dispatch and delivery dates. Once a refund is issued, record that issue date and the method named. Counting from the day the original parcel arrived can make a payment appear overdue even when the refund decision happened much later. The public refund policy itself distinguishes time spent reviewing a request from time needed for the credit to appear.

Use the time range in the message attached to your transaction, including whether it counts working days. If the message gives no range, ask the official support team which date and method it is using. Check the transaction history for the named destination before asking for an update. If you see the correct amount there, record it. If the platform says it issued the refund but the payment provider cannot find it, request the refund reference, issue date and amount so the provider can trace the same transaction.

Ask for the one detail that is still missing

A useful follow-up can fit into a few sentences. For instance, state that the order contained two shirts, that the return of the blue shirt was delivered on the date shown by the carrier, and that the case still shows no refund decision. Ask whether the parcel has been matched to your case and whether anything else is needed. At the payment stage, replace the parcel details with the amount, date and payment destination. Keep the reply in the existing conversation so it remains attached to the evidence.

Close your record when the actual outcome matches the decision. That might mean money received at the stated destination, an alternative you accepted or a written explanation of an unresolved difference. Keep the final message beside the original receipt so you can reconcile a partial amount later. For a future order from the same creator or shop, keep its new evidence separate. A return instruction, address or refund route issued for the first purchase gives you no reliable instructions for the second.
