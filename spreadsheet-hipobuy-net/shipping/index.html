No invented flat rates

Build a shipping estimate from the right inputs.

International shipping cannot be reduced to one universal price. Destination, route, parcel dimensions, actual weight, item restrictions and optional services can all affect the quote.

CHARGEABLE WEIGHT

Actual weight vs volumetric weight

Some routes charge the larger of the parcel’s measured weight and a dimensional calculation. The divisor and rounding method can vary, so use the live estimator for the selected route rather than copying a rate from another parcel.

01

Item subtotal

Record the current listing price, selected variant and any domestic delivery to the warehouse.

02

Parcel build

Estimate consolidated contents, protective packing and whether boxes materially increase dimensions.

03

Route eligibility

Filter for destination, item type and restrictions before comparing speed or headline price.

04

Final quote

Use measured warehouse data and the live quote. Treat earlier values only as planning references.

Do not promise delivery dates from an estimate.

Processing, carrier handoff, customs and last-mile delivery are separate stages. Keep the tracking record and allow for route-specific uncertainty.
