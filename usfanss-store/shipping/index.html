Is product payment the final cost?

No. A cross-border agent order normally has at least two cost stages: purchasing the item and later paying for the international parcel. Optional services, packaging, route rules and billable weight can affect the second stage.

06

How should I compare international shipping routes?

Use the final packed weight and dimensions, destination, restrictions, estimated delivery range and available tracking—not only the lowest headline price. The route available for one parcel may not be available for another.

07

Are QC photos a guarantee of quality or authenticity?

No. Photos are visual evidence of the item received at the warehouse. They help detect visible colour, size, construction or damage issues, but cannot prove materials, long-term durability or authenticity.

08

What should a first-time buyer do before parcel submission?

Save the original listing, confirm the selected variant, inspect all warehouse photos promptly, resolve returns within the displayed window, remove unwanted items, then compare shipping routes using the final parcel information.
