What happens after a seller sends an item?

The public product-page workflow says the item goes to the USFans warehouse, where staff check it and take pictures. The customer can review those images before deciding whether to keep, return or later submit the item for international shipping.

06

How many QC photos are provided?

USFans product pages currently advertise three to seven free HD inspection photos. The exact angles can vary by item, so buyers should still compare every available image with the seller listing.

07

Can an item be returned from the warehouse?

USFans product pages currently state that a return can be requested within five days after warehouse arrival if the buyer is dissatisfied. Acceptance can still depend on the seller and the item’s after-sales conditions, so inspect promptly.

08

Why can seller delivery to the warehouse take longer than expected?

USFans has publicly explained that delivery to its warehouse is controlled by the third-party seller. An estimated warehouse-arrival time is therefore not the same as a guaranteed purchasing or dispatch deadline.
