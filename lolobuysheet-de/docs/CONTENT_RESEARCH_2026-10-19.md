### Facts requiring careful qualification

- Photos: standard inspection says 3–6 item QC images, while the service overview says 5 plus 5 on request and separately parcel pictures. Preserve source-specific descriptions; no unconditional number for all orders. The no-inspection option supplies no QC photos.
- Storage: dedicated storage and current status pages say 180 days; buying guide says 90. Abnormal unmatched parcels have a separate 30-day statement. The extension fee unit is unclear; no invented per-item rate.
- Returns: replace ambiguity around the old cancellation page's “5 days after” with the dedicated eligible 120-hour rule, while preserving eligibility and actual-order confirmation. This is not a statutory consumer-rights article.
- Claims: 7/45-day text and 3-day warning still conflict. Recommend prompt case-specific action, not a newly invented consolidated cutoff.
- Weight: do not copy the official malformed numerical example. Editorial arithmetic uses clearly hypothetical values and correctly labels dimensions, increments and currencies.
- Sizing: 29W × 30L inch conversion is a label explanation, not a finished-measurement guarantee. Larger-foot/afternoon guidance and manual variance do not justify a universal size adjustment.
