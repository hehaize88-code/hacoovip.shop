Quick answers

Before you use any spreadsheet

What is Sugargoos.de?

Sugargoos.de is an independent product-link directory and research guide for people comparing China shopping links, Sugargoo workflows, warehouse QC and shipping to Germany. It does not sell, purchase, store, inspect or ship goods. Product, category and search actions lead to the cnfanshp.com main catalogue.

Is Sugargoos.de an official Sugargoo website?

No. Sugargoos.de is not owned, operated or endorsed by Sugargoo. Platform facts are researched from current official public materials and named as text, but account support, payments, purchasing, warehouse actions, shipping and refunds must be handled in the user's own platform account.

What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?

It is usually an independently organised collection of product links intended to make discovery faster. A spreadsheet is an index, not inventory or a quality guarantee. Open the live product record, check the exact variant and price, and review your own warehouse evidence before international parcel submission.

Where do the product and category buttons go?

They open matching records or categories on the cnfanshp.com main catalogue. The site does not use shopping buttons to send visitors to Sugargoo's public website. A destination can still change after the visible last-check date, so confirm the live page before paying.

Does the search box search cnfanshp.com?

Yes. A submitted keyword is carried to the corresponding cnfanshp.com search results. The category and budget controls refine the product records shown on this site, while a product card opens the matching main-catalogue detail page.
