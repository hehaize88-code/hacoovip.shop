Editorial standard

An independent finder with a documented method

Sugargoos.de is designed to make product-link research more useful than a raw spreadsheet while staying honest about what a link directory can prove.

What we are

Research layer, not retailer or shopping agent

We organize and explain links that lead to the main product catalog at cnfanshp.com. We do not accept orders, process payments, hold inventory, provide warehouse inspection or arrange international delivery.

Sugargoo is referenced because shoppers search for its workflow, QC photos and shipping process. This site is not official and is not affiliated with Sugargoo.

  1. 01

    Destination check

    We confirm that the cnfanshp.com record loads and note its visible title, item ID, CNY price and category.

  2. 02

    Language cleanup

    We write a readable neutral label without turning a seller's promotional words into our own quality claim.

  3. 03

    QC plan

    We add category-specific measurements and visible checks that can inform a warehouse decision.

  4. 04

    Boundary and date

    We show when the link was checked and explain that price, stock, legality, seller behavior and physical quality are not guaranteed.

Source policy

Platform facts need platform sources

For claims such as the usual number of free QC photos, we cite official Sugargoo material and record a review date. For shipping price, route eligibility, delivery windows and policy, we direct readers to live data instead of freezing a number in an article.

Independent research site. Sugargoos.de is not affiliated with Sugargoo or the brands named in catalog records. A listed link, image or title is not an authenticity or quality claim.
