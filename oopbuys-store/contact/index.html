What belongs somewhere else

Questions about an order, payment, refund, warehouse inspection, shipping line or delivery claim must go to the company that accepted that transaction. Include the relevant order or parcel number in that service’s own support channel. Sending those records to this publication will not give us access to the account or speed up a resolution.

How correction requests are reviewed

  1. We compare the disputed wording with the source record and the date on which it was checked.
  2. We distinguish a changed policy from an error in the original article.
  3. Material corrections are made in the article and reflected in its updated date.
  4. Requests that lack a page URL or supporting evidence may require more information before a decision.

Messages are reviewed as resources allow; no response time is guaranteed. Good-faith privacy and rights notices are prioritized.
