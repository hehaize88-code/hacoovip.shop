OOPBUY FAQ · FACT CHECKED 26 JUL 2026

QC photos, shipping, storage and returns—explained.

Practical answers based on OOPBuy’s public beginner guide, inspection rules, warehouse-service table, storage notice, return policy and shipping guidance.

What was checked

We reviewed the public rules for ordering, warehouse inspection, detailed photos, storage, returns, parcel services, shipping and customs. Policy facts are summarized here without linking visitors away.

How does OOPBuy ordering work, and why are there two payments?+

OOPBuy’s beginner material separates the agent process into two stages. First, the buyer pays for the product and any seller-to-warehouse delivery in China. After the item is stored, the buyer consolidates eligible goods, selects a route and pays international shipping. The source price is therefore not the delivered total.

Which marketplace links does OOPBuy say it can handle?+

OOPBuy publicly describes itself as an agent for Taobao, 1688 and Weidian. A link still has to load, show purchasable information and comply with platform, warehouse, carrier and destination rules; some listings can be rejected or require confirmation.

What happens when a seller’s parcel reaches the OOPBuy warehouse?+

The public order-status guidance separates arrival from storage. A parcel may first be signed for, then inspected and weighed, and only later become stored. When the item is shown as stored, it can appear in the warehouse area for photo review and parcel submission.

What does OOPBuy’s warehouse inspection check?+

The inspection notice says staff check quantity, color, the visible size or specification and appearance for damage. It also limits the scope: fit, exact color difference, use effect, hidden interiors, authenticity, material composition and professional electronic functions are not guaranteed by a normal receiving inspection.

When can I see OOPBuy QC photos?+

The beginner and status guidance place inspection after warehouse receipt. Photos belong to the received unit only after intake and inspection are completed; use the stored warehouse record rather than a domestic tracking event as the decision point.

How many regular OOPBuy inspection photos are provided?+

OOPBuy’s fine-photography notice says regular inspection generally provides three ordinary-pixel views: front, back and side, or a logo view where appropriate. The exact framing can vary with the product.

How much is an extra detailed OOPBuy photo?+

The published warehouse-services table lists detailed photos at CNY 1 each, requested either with the product order or after storage. The fine-photography notice tells buyers to state the precise angle, position, measurement or label needed and make the requested photo count match the instructions.

Do OOPBuy QC photos guarantee quality or authenticity?+

No. Photos can reveal a wrong visible variant, size label, missing part, stain, tear, dent or obvious construction issue. They cannot prove authenticity, fiber content, comfort, internal electronics, long-term durability or details outside the photographed area.

How long does OOPBuy provide free warehouse storage?+

OOPBuy publishes a 90-day free storage period. Its warehouse-services table lists an extension at CNY 10 per product ID per month for eligible items in the 60-to-90-day storage window and describes a six-month maximum. The live item timer and current purchase screen should govern if wording changes.

What is OOPBuy’s current warehouse return window?+

The current return-and-exchange notice describes a five-day window—120 hours—after an item becomes stored, counted from the next full hour. No-reason return or exchange still depends on the source seller, product eligibility, exclusions and China-side return costs, so inspect promptly.

Can an OOPBuy order be changed after the seller ships?+

OOPBuy’s public guidance says it may negotiate a change before seller dispatch. Once the seller has shipped, a change generally cannot be negotiated at that stage; the buyer may need to wait for warehouse receipt, request a return if eligible and pay the resulting domestic delivery cost.

What costs should I budget beyond the product price?+

Plan for the item, seller-to-warehouse delivery when charged, payment or currency-conversion costs, optional warehouse services, international freight and possible tax or customs charges. OOPBuy’s public service page separates optional value-added services, so the amount beside a product should never be treated as a landed price.

Why can OOPBuy’s final shipping cost differ from the estimate?+

Early estimates use preliminary parcel data. The payable figure can change with packed dimensions, actual or volumetric weight, destination, eligible route, restricted-item classification and optional services. OOPBuy’s tutorial says an underpaid shipping amount requires a supplementary payment.

What does OOPBuy rehearsal packing cost and do?+

The published value-added table lists rehearsal packing at CNY 20 per parcel. The warehouse packs the selected items, weighs the resulting parcel and sends the result by account message and email, giving the buyer better parcel data before the final shipping decision.

What does the OOPBuy parcel-photo service include?+

The same table lists parcel photos at CNY 5 per parcel. It describes one image before sealing and one after sealing, with the result sent through account message and email. These photos document external packing; they do not replace item-level QC.

What should I know about OOPBuy customs and route restrictions?+

OOPBuy’s customs guidance says heavy, high-value parcels or many identical items can increase the chance of inspection. A carrier or customs authority may ask the recipient for an invoice or clearance documents, and restricted goods may be ineligible for a route. Taxes, inspection and clearance decisions are controlled by the destination authorities, not by a spreadsheet.

How do I track an OOPBuy parcel after dispatch?+

The public tracking guidance directs users to User Center, My Package and Logistics Details. It also warns that some routes may take about three days to show the first event and that a later scan can take roughly three to ten days, depending on the line.

Is oopbuys.store an official OOPBuy site or a seller-verification service?+

No. oopbuys.store is an independent product-discovery and buyer-education site. Link destinations and sample images can be checked, but that does not certify seller reliability, live stock, authenticity or product quality. Re-check the source listing and warehouse evidence before shipping.
