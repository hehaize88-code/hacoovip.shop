WAREHOUSE DECISION GUIDE

Use QC photos well. Treat shipping estimates carefully.

Warehouse images help with visible checks; route estimates help with planning. Neither removes uncertainty, so this guide separates what you can verify from what still needs confirmation.

Check QC photosEstimate shippingFollow a parcel
Fast QC checklist
  • Correct item and selected variant
  • Size label and visible measurements
  • Color under warehouse lighting
  • Stains, tears, dents or missing parts
  • Packaging condition
Read common questions →

What QC photos can—and cannot—show

OOPBuy’s public fine-photography notice says regular inspection generally provides three ordinary views: front, back and side or logo. Those images can confirm broad appearance, received color, an exposed size tag and some visible defects, but not durability, exact material composition, internal electronics, comfort or authenticity. Its published warehouse table lists targeted detail photos at CNY 1 each; confirm the live price before ordering.

Useful comparison points

  • Silhouette, color blocking and obvious print placement
  • Quantity and included accessories
  • Visible stitching, surface damage and symmetry
  • Size label versus the ordered variant
  • Measured dimensions when an image is available

Storage is time, not a reason to delay forever

Current OOPBuy product pages display “90 Days Free Storage.” One public storage page lists an extension at CNY 10 per 30 days, while the value-added table says CNY 10 per product ID per month. Confirm the remaining time and live charge in your account because the public wording is not identical.

The practical sequence is simple: inspect soon after intake, consolidate only items you want, compare routes using current parcel data, then submit before storage becomes a problem. Use the warehouse storage and returns guide to track the 120-hour after-sales clock separately from the 90-day storage clock.
