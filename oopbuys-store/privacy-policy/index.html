PRIVACY POLICY

A plain-language explanation of data handling.

oopbuys.store is a static informational site. It does not offer user accounts, checkout, comments or an on-site contact form, and it does not ask readers for payment details.

oopbuys.store is a static informational site. It does not offer user accounts, checkout, comments or an on-site contact form, and it does not ask readers for payment details.

Information processed when you visit

The hosting and content-delivery infrastructure may automatically process ordinary request data such as an IP address, requested URL, browser or device information, timestamp and security or diagnostic signals. This processing is used to deliver pages, prevent abuse and maintain availability.

The site does not currently embed advertising trackers or ask visitors to create a profile. If measurement or advertising tools are added later, this policy will be updated before those uses are represented as current.

Email and voluntary information

If you email the contact address, the message may include your email address and any information you choose to provide. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or unrelated order records. Messages may be retained only as reasonably needed to review the request, keep a correction record, protect rights or meet applicable obligations.

External links

Product, category and search links open a separate catalog or service. That destination applies its own privacy terms and may collect information when you visit, sign in or transact. This privacy policy does not control third-party sites.

Your request

You may ask whether information from an email request is held, or request correction or deletion where applicable. Send the request through the contact page and identify the message or subject involved. We may need enough information to distinguish the request from another person’s records.

Children and policy changes

This publication is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. Material changes to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date. The current effective date is 2026-07-28.
