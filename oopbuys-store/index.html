QUICK ANSWERS

Know this before ordering.

View all FAQs→
How many regular OOPBuy QC photos are provided?+

OOPBuy’s fine-photography notice says regular inspection generally provides three ordinary views: front, back and side or logo.

How much does an extra detailed photo cost?+

The published warehouse-services table lists a requested detailed photo at CNY 1 each. State the exact angle or measurement you need.

How long is OOPBuy’s free warehouse storage?+

OOPBuy publishes a 90-day free storage period. Check the item timer in the account before relying on the deadline.

Why can final shipping cost differ from the estimate?+

Packed dimensions, actual or volumetric weight, destination, eligible route and optional services can change the final charge.
