Frequently asked questions

The essentials to understand before opening a listing or using the index.

What is USFans?+

USFans presents itself as a shopping-agent service that purchases products from Chinese marketplaces and receives them at a warehouse before international parcel shipping.

Which marketplace links does USFans publicly support?+

The USFans home page names Taobao, 1688, and Weidian and describes a paste-the-link ordering flow.

When does USFans purchase paid orders?+

Current USFans product pages state that purchasing is handled from 09:00 to 18:00 China time (UTC+8) after payment. Seller dispatch time is separate.

How many free QC photos are provided?+

Current USFans product pages advertise 3–7 free HD quality-inspection photos after warehouse arrival.

Can I return an item after seeing warehouse photos?+

USFans product pages currently say buyers can apply for a return within five days after warehouse arrival and that agents negotiate with the seller. This is an application process, not a guarantee that every seller accepts every return.

Is the spreadsheet price the delivered price?+

No. The listing price is only one layer. Domestic delivery, packaging or services, international freight, and possible destination charges can affect the delivered total.

How does USFans calculate volumetric weight?+

The public estimator gives the formula length × width × height in centimetres ÷ 6000. It says chargeable weight is the higher of actual and volumetric weight.

Do QC photos prove authenticity or durability?+

No. They can document visible condition and order identity in the supplied views, but they do not prove authenticity, hidden construction, material composition, or long-term performance.

How many countries does USFans ship to?+

The current USFans home page advertises shipping to more than 50 countries. Actual route availability depends on destination, parcel, product category, and current carrier rules.

Does this independent directory sell or inspect the products?+

No. This site organises product discovery and research. Always verify the current listing, warehouse evidence, parcel quote, and applicable terms on the service you use.
