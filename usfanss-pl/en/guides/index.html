A better route

From inspiration to the product page in three steps.

Start with search or a category, compare a short list, then open the full detail page and verify the current information.

  1. 01

    Open the current marketplace link

    USFans supports pasted product links from Taobao, 1688, and Weidian. Verify the live listing instead of relying only on a spreadsheet snapshot.

  2. 02

    Confirm the exact option

    Match variant, size, colour, quantity, price, and domestic delivery before payment. USFans product pages currently state purchases are processed from 09:00–18:00 (UTC+8).

  3. 03

    Wait for warehouse intake

    The marketplace seller sends the item within China to the USFans warehouse; this is separate from the later international parcel.

  4. 04

    Review QC promptly

    USFans product pages advertise 3–7 free HD inspection photos and currently describe a five-day return-application window after warehouse arrival.

  5. 05

    Estimate and submit the parcel

    Compare actual weight with volumetric weight. USFans publishes the formula L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6000 and uses the higher figure as chargeable weight.
