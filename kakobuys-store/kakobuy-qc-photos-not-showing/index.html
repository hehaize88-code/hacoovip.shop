Kakobuy QC Photos Not Showing After Delivery? Start Here

Find out why your Kakobuy QC photos are missing after delivery. Check the order record, diagnose blank images and ask for the evidence you need before shipping.

If a seller says your item has arrived but you cannot see any QC photos, open the individual order in your Kakobuy account. Look for a warehouse record that matches the item and the option you bought. What you find there will tell you whether to trace an incoming delivery, ask about an inspection, report a broken image or request one extra view. Keep the item out of an international parcel while you are waiting for evidence that could change your decision.

01

First, find the record that belongs to you

A shopping link, a domestic tracking number and a warehouse item reference answer different questions. The link identifies what the seller listed. Domestic tracking follows the package on its way to the warehouse. The item reference connects the piece in storage with your purchase. Start with the order confirmation, open its details and copy the identifiers exactly as they appear. Keep them private when asking a public community for advice.

If you ordered two sizes of the same hoodie, the title and thumbnail may look identical on both rows. Match the selected size and colour before opening either gallery. A link to a seller's current listing may now open a different default option. Your saved order details are the better record of the option you selected. For a forwarded purchase, also look for the domestic tracking reference you entered when you registered the parcel.

02

The carrier says delivered, but there is no warehouse entry

A delivery scan tells you what the carrier recorded about a parcel. It cannot tell you which item was inside, whether the warehouse has associated it with your account or whether somebody has photographed it. Open the tracking details and note the delivery time. Then look for a warehouse entry against the same purchase. If the seller split the order into two domestic parcels, check both tracking numbers before reporting that one item has disappeared.

Suppose a shirt and a cap share one seller order. The cap already has photographs. The shirt has a delivery scan but no item entry in your account. Sending the cap's warehouse reference could send support to a perfectly healthy record. Give the seller order, the shirt's own details and the tracking number that is supposed to contain the shirt. Ask which receiving record, if any, matches it. Keep the reference for the cap separate so you can explain the difference if needed.

03

Your item is in storage, but the photo area is empty

Read the status and any staff note beside the exact item. A warehouse entry can establish that an item is recorded without telling you whether its photo set has been attached. Save a screenshot that shows the item reference, selected option, current status and empty area together. If that takes two screenshots, include the same reference in each. This gives support enough information to identify the issue even if the display changes later.

Ask whether an inspection set exists for that item, whether you are looking in the right place for your order type, and what the next step is if the set is missing. A photograph available for somebody else's order does not answer those questions. If the page shows an after-sales deadline, include it in your message and ask what action preserves your options. An open chat or a pending photo request does not establish that a deadline has moved.

04

There are thumbnails, but the full photos will not open

This symptom is worth reporting precisely because the warehouse may already have taken the pictures. Try opening a second thumbnail from the same item. If one photograph opens and one stays blank, record which view fails. If none open, try a normal refresh and reopen the order through your account. If convenient, compare the same item in a second supported browser while signed into your own account.

Describe what you actually saw. “Both overview photos open, but tapping the size-label thumbnail shows a blank panel” is more informative than “no QC”. Tell support which device or browser showed the problem and whether the same picture opened elsewhere. Crop unrelated personal information out of any screen capture. A public album cannot fix a private gallery display problem, and a buyer should never need to give another shopper a password or sign-in code to troubleshoot it.

05

The photos opened, but you still cannot decide

Name the decision you need to make. If you cannot tell whether the warehouse received the right model, request a readable product label beside the item. If a shoe's fit is uncertain, ask whether the insole is removable and whether a photograph can show the heel and toe ends against the same scale. For a jacket with a possible stain, ask for one close-up and one wider view that shows where the mark lies.

For example, “Please photograph the hoodie laid flat, with the tape running straight from one underarm seam to the other and both ends visible” gives the photographer a repeatable task. “Please take better QC” leaves them guessing. Check the service options, any charge and the expected availability in your order before confirming a request. Once the new photograph arrives, compare it with the seller's measurement for that same size and method.

06

A public QC finder shows pictures, but your order has none

A public result can show what another buyer received from a listing at another time. It can help you decide which detail to inspect later. It cannot establish who owns the photographed piece or whether the warehouse has completed your own inspection. Even a familiar background and an identical-looking label are insufficient to connect a public photograph to your order.

Use the difference to make your question sharper. If a public picture shows a removable strap and your bag's overview does not, check whether your selected option included the strap. Then ask for a photo of the actual bag with the supplied parts laid beside it. Until you see your item or receive a clear answer about its contents, keep the public image with your research and the warehouse correspondence with your order.

07

Send one message that a warehouse colleague can act on

For an unlinked delivery, adapt this message. “My order [order reference], item [name and option], has domestic tracking [reference]. The carrier recorded delivery on [date and time shown]. I cannot find a matching warehouse item in my account. Please check whether the parcel has been associated with this order and tell me the next step.”

For an existing item with no visible set, use a shorter request. “Warehouse item [reference] shows [status]. The photo area is [empty / showing thumbnails that fail to open]. I checked [what you tried]. Could you confirm whether inspection photos exist for this item and where I can view them? The order shows [deadline, if present].” Keep the reply in the same conversation so that a second colleague can follow the record.

08

Decide what counts as resolved

Return to the same item after the reply. Check that the photographs open, identify the ordered option and show enough evidence for the decision in front of you. If support restored a gallery but the only size-label image is unreadable, the display problem is resolved and the remaining task is a specific label request. Make that distinction when you reply.

If a deadline or parcel-packing step is approaching, check the live status again before assuming the item is still available for inspection. Ask whether packing can be held and wait for confirmation of what the warehouse can actually do. When everything matches, save the useful views and continue with your parcel. If a detail clearly conflicts with the order, keep the original photo and the selected option together for an after-sales request.
