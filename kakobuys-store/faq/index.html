FAQ / QUICK ANSWERS

Questions before you browse.

Clear answers about this independent index, destination links, prices and QC checks.
01What does this site do?+

It organizes product-discovery routes, QC guidance and direct links to matching current listings.

Related guide →
02Are prices guaranteed?+

No. Displayed USD prices are references. Confirm the live price, variants and availability on the destination page.

Related guide →
03What should I check in warehouse photos?+

Review shape, color, measurements, stitching, print placement, hardware and any detail important to your order.

Related guide →
04Do category links open the correct category?+

Yes. Each category shortcut opens its matching live category rather than a generic search.

Related guide →
05Why can a displayed price change?+

Product prices, variants, seller discounts and availability can change after a record is checked. Treat the displayed USD amount as a comparison reference and confirm the current total on the destination page.

Related guide →
06Is the product price the final parcel cost?+

No. The item price does not include every possible cost. Domestic delivery, packed weight, parcel dimensions, the selected international route and destination charges can affect the final amount.

Related guide →
07How should I choose a clothing size?+

Compare the seller's measurements with a similar item you own. Do not rely only on S, M, L or a familiar local size because size labels can vary between sellers and products.

Related guide →
08Can I rely on one QC photo?+

No. Review several angles and compare shape, color, measurements and details. Lighting and camera angle can make a single image misleading, especially for color and proportions.

Related guide →
09What if an important detail is missing from the QC photos?+

Write down the exact area you need checked and request a focused photo when that option is available. A precise request such as outsole length or zipper hardware is more useful than asking for a general recheck.

Related guide →
10Why might a product link become unavailable?+

Marketplace sellers can remove a listing, change its variants or replace its content. If a destination no longer matches the card, do not continue until you have found and verified the correct live record.

Related guide →
11How does the search box work?+

It sends your keywords to the connected catalog search. Use a product type plus one useful constraint, such as color, style or budget, for more focused results.

Related guide →
12When should I review return or exchange options?+

Check them before ordering and again as soon as the item reaches the warehouse. Eligibility, seller rules and deadlines can vary, so do not wait until parcel submission to investigate a problem.

Related guide →
13How should I use warehouse storage time?+

Inspect every arrival immediately, resolve return or exchange questions before their deadline, and use the remaining storage time to compare parcel groups, measured weight, dimensions and shipping routes.
