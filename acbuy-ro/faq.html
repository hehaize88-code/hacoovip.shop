ACBuy questions about orders, QC and shipping

Follow an order from the original product link through checkout, warehouse photos and international shipping.

What is ACBuy?

ACBuy is a shopping-agent platform that helps users purchase eligible products from Chinese domestic marketplaces, receive them at a warehouse and later submit an international parcel.

Is ACBuy.ro the official ACBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery and educational website. Platform accounts, payments, orders, warehouse services and shipping are handled on ACBuy’s own website.

Which marketplace links can ACBuy retrieve?

ACBuy’s official shopping guide says its system can retrieve information for 99% of Taobao, Tmall and 1688 products. Retrieval does not guarantee that every listing is purchasable or eligible for every shipping route.

Why did my product link open a DIY order page?

According to ACBuy’s shopping guide, a DIY page means the product requires a manual agent purchase. Enter the source link, price, quantity and exact variant carefully.

Do I pay international shipping when I buy the product?

Usually the product purchase and international parcel are separate stages. International freight is selected and paid after stored items are prepared as a parcel.

What do ACBuy order statuses mean?

Statuses generally track purchasing, seller dispatch, domestic delivery, warehouse processing and storage. A domestic shipping status does not mean the item has started international transit.

How many free QC photos does ACBuy provide?

ACBuy’s public homepage advertises 3–5 free QC photos. Check the live order page for the images and any current extra-photo options attached to a specific item.

What can QC photos confirm?

They can help confirm visible colour, size labels, quantity, general design and obvious damage. They cannot prove authenticity, material composition, internal construction or long-term durability.

How long is free warehouse storage?

ACBuy’s homepage advertises 90 days of free storage. Verify the current deadline shown in your account and avoid waiting until the final days to submit a parcel.

Can several orders be shipped together?

Stored items can be selected for parcel consolidation, subject to product compatibility and route rules. One large parcel is not automatically cheaper if volume or restrictions increase the charge.

Why can the final shipping price differ from the estimator?

Packed weight, dimensions, optional packaging and current carrier pricing can differ from early assumptions. ACBuy also notes that third-party logistics providers may adjust freight prices.

What is volumetric weight?

It is a carrier calculation representing the space occupied by a parcel. Some routes bill the greater of actual and volumetric weight, making large lightweight boxes more expensive than expected.

Does ACBuy offer one shipping line for every parcel?

No. ACBuy promotes 150+ lines platform-wide, but the eligible list depends on destination, parcel measurements, product classification and current carrier restrictions.

Which ACBuy shipping route is best?

There is no universal best route. Compare the live options for price, billing method, delivery estimate, tracking, accepted contents, size limits and compensation terms.

Can I return an item after warehouse inspection?

Eligibility depends on seller policy, product category, reason and timing. If QC reveals a problem, use the after-sales options shown for the order promptly.

Should I remove product boxes to reduce shipping cost?

Removing bulky retail boxes can reduce volume, but it also reduces protection. Decide according to fragility, route billing and whether the box matters to you.

Does an available route guarantee customs clearance?

No. Import taxes, declarations and prohibited-product rules belong to the destination country. Route availability is not a guarantee of tax-free or inspection-free delivery.

Are product and shipping prices on this site final?

No. Any displayed amount is for discovery or comparison. Verify current product prices, exchange rates, fees and parcel quotes on the destination platform before paying.

Where should a first-time ACBuy user begin?

Start with a modest item from a clear listing. Learn the purchasing, warehouse, QC and parcel workflow before combining a large number of orders.

How can I find the original listing if I only have a short ACBuy link?

Open the link and look for a source listing on the resulting product page. If you can open the seller’s page, check the marketplace, item identifier and selected option there. A short code alone may not reveal the original listing.

Can I search ACBuy with just a photo?

ACBuy’s shopping guide describes image search as a way to find similar items. Start with a crop showing the whole object, then compare photographs of the seller’s selected variant. A second angle showing a distinctive feature can help you choose.
