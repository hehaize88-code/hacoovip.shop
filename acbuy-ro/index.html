FAQ

All questions
What is ACBuy?

ACBuy is a shopping-agent platform that helps users purchase eligible products from Chinese domestic marketplaces, receive them at a warehouse and later submit an international parcel.

Is ACBuy.ro the official ACBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery and educational website. Platform accounts, payments, orders, warehouse services and shipping are handled on ACBuy’s own website.

Which marketplace links can ACBuy retrieve?

ACBuy’s official shopping guide says its system can retrieve information for 99% of Taobao, Tmall and 1688 products. Retrieval does not guarantee that every listing is purchasable or eligible for every shipping route.
