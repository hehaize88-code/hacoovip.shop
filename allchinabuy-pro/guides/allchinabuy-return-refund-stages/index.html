After-sales records

AllChinaBuy Returns and Refunds: Order and Parcel Stages

Organise an AllChinaBuy cancellation, warehouse return or parcel enquiry with matching records, and distinguish a requested refund from a completed credit.

Key points

Before you begin

  1. Cancellation, seller return, parcel investigation and completed refund are different events.
  2. Eligibility, deductions, deadlines and payment destinations must be confirmed for the actual transaction.
  3. The October 5 maintenance notice provides no order-specific refund promise or completion date.

Section 01

Identify which process you need

Searching for an AllChinaBuy refund can describe several different problems. You may want to cancel an unpurchased item, return goods held in a warehouse, correct an amount, investigate a parcel, or understand a credit already shown in your account. These processes need different evidence and may involve different parties. Begin with the last confirmed stage, the specific issue and the remedy you want. A single word such as ‘refund’ is not enough to show what happened.

This guide does not publish a universal AllChinaBuy refund policy or promise that a request will be accepted. The public homepage showed a maintenance notice on October 5, 2026, and the current account-only rules could not be independently verified. The practical method below is designed to organise your records and questions. Keep the seller's terms, the platform transaction and your payment provider's process separate instead of assuming that one automatically determines the others.

Section 02

Before seller purchase: confirm whether cancellation is possible

A payment receipt confirms a payment event, not necessarily that an agent has completed the seller purchase. If you want to cancel, ask whether the purchase has been placed and whether the order can still be stopped. Save the order reference, selected option, quantity, amount and date. If an order screen is available, preserve its exact status and the cancellation options displayed. Do not treat a disabled button as proof that no support process exists, or a visible button as proof that the cancellation has succeeded.

After sending the request, look for a confirmation that identifies the order and action. A message acknowledging receipt is different from an approved cancellation. Likewise, approval is different from a completed financial credit. Record each stage separately. If a second order was placed accidentally, disclose both references and explain which one you intended to keep. Do not create another payment or replacement order solely because the first request is slow to receive a response.

Section 03

At the warehouse: document the reason and preserve the item

For an item already received, compare the selected option with the available QC evidence. A wrong colour, missing component, visible defect and an unexpected fit are different reasons for a request. Describe the observable issue precisely and attach the relevant image or measurement. A statement that a seam appears open is easier to investigate than an unsupported claim that everything is poor quality. If the photograph is ambiguous, request clarification before describing the ambiguity as a confirmed defect.

Ask whether a seller return is available, which deadline applies, whether packaging or tags must be preserved, and which charges would be deducted or remain non-refundable. This article cannot set those terms. Avoid authorising repacking, disposal of packaging or international shipment while a return decision is unresolved, unless the responsible support team explains the consequences and you accept them. Save the response so your next action can be traced to the actual instruction for that item.

  • Order and warehouse item references
  • Selected variant and relevant QC evidence
  • Specific reason for the request
  • Confirmed return deadline, condition requirements and possible deductions

Section 04

After parcel submission: establish custody first

Once goods have been allocated to a parcel, a request may require a packing change, a shipment stop or a carrier investigation. First establish whether the parcel is only created, packed, handed to a carrier or already in transit. A parcel reference alone does not answer that question. Ask what action is still possible at the confirmed stage and whether it has a cost. Do not assume a domestic seller-return procedure can be applied unchanged to an international parcel.

If goods arrive damaged or incomplete, retain the packaging, shipping label and item evidence while you ask about the relevant process. Photograph the received condition honestly and distinguish exterior damage, missing contents and an incorrect product. Keep any unboxing record you already have; do not reconstruct an event and present it as original evidence. A carrier delivery confirmation may establish delivery of a package but does not, by itself, resolve a dispute about its contents.

Section 05

Reconcile the money in separate rows

Build a ledger with the original product amount, domestic delivery, service charges, international freight and every subsequent credit or adjustment. Use the recorded currency and date for each row. An account balance, an authorised refund and a settled credit at your bank are different financial records. Ask where an approved amount will be credited, whether any further withdrawal step is required and which transaction reference identifies completion. Never assume that a credit automatically returns to the original payment instrument.

A hypothetical example illustrates the bookkeeping rather than platform policy. If your record contains a product payment of 40 units and a separate delivery payment of 5 units, a credit of 40 does not establish what happened to the other 5. Mark the delivery row unresolved until the response explains it. If conversion or processing differences appear, preserve the original and credited currencies and request a breakdown. Do not label every difference an improper fee without checking the corresponding transaction terms.

Practical takeaway

Requested, approved and credited should be separate entries in your refund timeline.

Section 06

Use one case file and a specific requested remedy

Put the essential evidence into a chronological file: order confirmation, payment, warehouse receipt, QC issue, return request, reply and financial outcome. Include only the stages that apply. Begin the message with the desired action, such as confirming cancellation, arranging an eligible return or explaining a missing credit. State the relevant references and describe what is still unknown. This makes it easier for a reviewer to match your question to the correct system record.

Save every case number and follow up within the same thread when possible. If you receive an answer that resolves only part of the issue, identify the remaining row rather than starting the explanation again. Keep copies of original documents and redact unrelated personal information in shared copies. Never send a card PIN, card security code, password or one-time login code as proof of a refund request. Use the official support route and verify unexpected requests independently.

Section 07

Check a hypothetical partial resolution

Consider an order containing a jacket and a cap. The jacket has a documented option mismatch, while the cap matches the selected listing. A request to return the jacket should identify its own order or item reference and specify whether the cap is to remain unchanged. If the two items are allocated to a planned parcel, ask whether the parcel needs to be revised before any dispatch. A message saying only ‘cancel my order’ may be too broad. Clear item-level instructions reduce the chance that the support response addresses the wrong part of the purchase.

Now imagine the response approves the jacket return but says nothing about domestic delivery charges. Record the approval, preserve any instructions for the return, and leave the unanswered charge row open. Do not mark the entire case complete simply because one message uses the word ‘refunded.’ Confirm the credited amount, currency, destination and transaction reference against the financial record. If the cap is later shipped, its parcel number belongs in a separate row. This scenario is an editorial example, not a claim about a customer's experience or a refund policy. It shows why the physical goods and the financial outcome need separate reconciliation.

Section 08

Handle maintenance and unresolved cases without guessing

A general maintenance announcement does not approve your refund, reject it or reset an applicable deadline. Record the access problem and the dates of attempts to obtain assistance. Consult the current published process of the service or payment provider responsible for the question. Available remedies and deadlines can depend on the transaction and jurisdiction, so this article does not prescribe a universal dispute period or guarantee a payment-provider outcome.

Close the file only when the practical and financial result is clear. A completed return should have an identified receiving or acceptance record where one is provided; a completed refund should have a traceable credit in the stated destination. If the result is partial, record what was settled and what remains open. Keeping product research, any new purchase and the unresolved AllChinaBuy transaction separate will make later reconciliation easier. The goal is an accurate record that supports a decision, not a promise based on another buyer's experience.
